# Content Type: Relocation Guide

## Definition

A Relocation Guide is the primary long-form content format for Paphos Advisors. Each guide is the definitive, standalone resource on a specific relocation topic, process, or journey stage. It is written for a clearly defined ICP segment and covers the subject comprehensively enough that a reader does not need to go elsewhere to understand the topic.

Guides serve dual purposes: they establish topical authority with search engines, and they build trust with prospective clients who are evaluating whether Paphos Advisors understands their situation.

---

## Target Length

**2,000–4,000 words**

- Minimum 2,000 words — guides below this threshold are not comprehensive enough to rank or build authority
- Maximum 4,000 words — beyond this, content should be split into multiple linked guides or a guide with sub-articles
- Aim for the word count that fully covers the topic — do not pad to hit a number, and do not truncate to stay under one

---

## Required Sections

Every Relocation Guide must include all of the following sections, in this order:

### 1. Introduction (100–200 words)
- Opens by acknowledging the reader's situation and what they are trying to accomplish
- States clearly what the guide covers and what the reader will know by the end
- Does NOT begin with "In this guide, we will..." — lead with the reader's context, not a table of contents
- Includes the primary keyword naturally in the first 100 words

### 2. Overview / What You Need to Know First (200–400 words)
- The single most important thing the reader needs to understand before proceeding
- Sets expectations: how complex is this, how long does it take, what does it cost
- Flags any eligibility conditions or prerequisites upfront — do not bury these

### 3. Eligibility / Who This Applies To (150–300 words)
- Clearly defines which reader this guide is for
- Flags differences by nationality, residency status, or circumstances where relevant
- If the guide applies to everyone (e.g. renting), still state this explicitly rather than omitting the section

### 4. Step-by-Step Process or Main Body (600–1,500 words)
- The core instructional content of the guide
- Numbered steps where the content is sequential; headed sections where it is not
- Each step must include: what to do, where to go, what documents or costs are involved, and what to expect
- Be specific — name the department, the form, the fee, the address. Vague guidance erodes trust.

### 5. Documents and Fees (200–400 words)
- A dedicated, scannable section listing every document and fee required
- Use a table or formatted list — do not embed this information only in prose
- State clearly: original vs copy, apostille requirements, translation requirements
- Include the date fees were last confirmed

### 6. Timeline (100–200 words)
- What is the realistic end-to-end timeline?
- Distinguish between official stated timeframes and real-world experience where they differ
- Flag any district-level variations if they exist (e.g. Paphos vs Limassol processing times)

### 7. Practical Tips (200–400 words)
- Field intelligence: what do people commonly get wrong, what do practitioners recommend
- Clearly marked as practical/field experience — not official guidance
- At least 3 specific tips; not generic advice

### 8. Related Topics / Next Steps (100–200 words)
- What process or topic typically follows this one?
- 3–5 internal links to related guides, process documents, or FAQs
- Ends with a clear call to action (see CTA requirements below)

## SEO Requirements

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Primary keyword | Must appear in: H1 title, first 100 words, at least one H2, meta description |
| Title tag | 50–60 characters; include primary keyword; do not stuff |
| Meta description | 140–155 characters; include primary keyword; written for clicks, not just crawlers |
| URL slug | kebab-case; primary keyword; max 5–6 words; no stop words |
| H1 | One only; matches or closely mirrors title tag |
| H2s | 4–8 per guide; include secondary keywords naturally; describe sections accurately |
| Internal links | Minimum 3; link to related process docs, guides, and service pages |
| External links | Link to official sources only (government portals, legislation); open in new tab |
| Schema markup | HowTo schema for step-by-step guides; FAQPage schema if FAQ section included |
| Image alt text | All images must have descriptive alt text including keyword where natural |
| Word count | 2,000–4,000 words as above |

**Keyword research requirement:** Every guide must have a defined primary keyword with a known search volume before briefing begins. Do not write guides without keyword validation.

---

## Source Requirements

| Source Type | Requirement |
|-------------|-------------|
| Official sources | At least one official source (government portal, legislation, regulatory body) must be cited for any factual claim about process, fees, or eligibility |
| Knowledge base | All factual claims must be linked to or derived from a validated knowledge article (KB-XXX) or process document (PROC-XXX) in the system |
| Field intelligence | Any field notes or practitioner insights must be clearly flagged as such — not presented as official guidance |
| Recency | No source older than 24 months should be cited without a note that it has been verified as still current |
| Prohibited sources | Do not cite other expat blogs, forums, or community posts as primary sources. These can inform research but cannot be cited as evidence |

**Minimum source requirement:** Every guide must be supported by at least one official source and at least one validated internal process document.

---

## Update Cadence

| Content Volatility | Review Frequency |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Evergreen (process unlikely to change) | Every 12 months |
| Volatile (fees, quotas, timelines, government policy) | Every 3 months |
| Mixed | Every 6 months — or immediately when a linked process document is updated |

**Trigger for immediate review:**
- A linked process document (PROC-XXX) is updated
- A regulatory change is announced in the relevant area
- A client or reader flags incorrect information
- An official source URL changes or returns a 404

**On review, check:**
- All fees and timelines are still current
- All external links are live and pointing to current pages
- All internal links still resolve correctly
- The process described matches the current linked process document
- The SEO title and meta description still reflect the content accurately

## Call to Action

Every Relocation Guide must end with a CTA. The CTA must be:
- Specific to the guide's topic (not a generic "contact us")
- Matched to where the reader is in their journey
- One primary CTA only — do not include multiple competing CTAs

**Standard CTAs by guide type:**

| Guide Type | Primary CTA |
|------------|-------------|
| Process guide (immigration, tax, property) | "Book a free consultation to discuss your specific situation" |
| Settling-in guide | "Download the [relevant] checklist" |
| Comparison guide | "Speak to an advisor to find the right route for you" |
| Lead magnet gateway guide | "Download the complete guide" |

## Quality Checklist

Before a guide is marked as "approved" for publication, verify:

- [ ] Word count is between 2,000 and 4,000
- [ ] All required sections are present and in order
- [ ] Primary keyword appears in H1, first 100 words, at least one H2, and meta description
- [ ] Title tag is 50–60 characters
- [ ] Meta description is 140–155 characters
- [ ] At least one official source is cited
- [ ] All factual claims are linked to a validated KB or PROC document
- [ ] Field intelligence is clearly marked as such
- [ ] Minimum 3 internal links included
- [ ] CTA is present, specific, and singular
- [ ] Fees include the date last confirmed
- [ ] HowTo or FAQPage schema is specified if applicable
