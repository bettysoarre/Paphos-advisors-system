# Content Type: Comparison Article

## Definition
A structured article that compares two or more options, routes, or scenarios to help a client make an informed decision. Optimised for decision-stage queries ("X vs Y", "best option for...").

## Examples

- "Category F vs Digital Nomad Visa — Which Route is Right for You?"
- "Renting vs Buying Property in Cyprus — Pros, Cons, and Costs"
- "60-Day Rule vs 183-Day Rule — Cyprus Tax Residency Options Explained"
- "Cyprus vs Malta for Company Formation — Tax, Cost, and Requirements Compared"
- "GESY vs Private Health Insurance in Cyprus"

## Word count
1,500–3,000 words

## Required sections

1. **Introduction** — frame the decision, who faces it, what this article will help them decide
2. **Quick comparison table** — at-a-glance summary of the key differences (appears early in the article, before the detail)
3. **Option A explained** — what it is, who it suits, key requirements, pros, cons
4. **Option B explained** — same structure
5. **Option C** (if applicable) — same structure
6. **Side-by-side comparison** — deeper table covering multiple dimensions (cost, eligibility, timeline, ongoing requirements, etc.)
7. **Decision guide** — "Choose Option A if...", "Choose Option B if..."
8. **Next steps** — links to the relevant process explainers for each option

## Rules

**Be genuinely helpful, not neutral.** Do not write false balance. If one option is clearly better for a specific client type, say so clearly. The "Decision guide" section should give real guidance, not hedge.

**Source every comparison point.** Each row in the comparison table must be traceable to a process doc or KB article.

**Note when conditions change.** Comparison articles go stale quickly if regulations change. Flag this explicitly in the update cadence.

## SEO requirements

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Meta title | "[Option A] vs [Option B] Cyprus — [qualifier]" |
| Primary keyword | The comparison query itself (e.g., "category f vs digital nomad visa cyprus") |
| Structured data | Article or FAQPage (if Q&A format) |

## Update cadence
Annual, or when either compared option has a regulatory change.

## Anti-cannibalisation
Do not create a comparison article for a pair of options that are already compared in a comprehensive guide on the same topic. The comparison article should be a standalone piece for decision-stage searchers.
