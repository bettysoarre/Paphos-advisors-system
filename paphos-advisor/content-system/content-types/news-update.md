# Content Type: News Update

## Definition
A short, timely piece covering a regulatory change, policy announcement, or process update that affects people relocating to or living in Cyprus. Published when relevant changes occur, not on a schedule.

## When to publish a news update

- A Cyprus law or regulation relevant to our topic areas has changed
- A government department has changed its procedures or requirements
- A deadline or transitional arrangement is approaching (e.g., end of a tax grace period)
- A significant court ruling affects process interpretation
- A major process has been suspended or reinstated

## When NOT to publish a news update

- General Cyprus news with no direct impact on relocation processes
- Changes that are rumoured but not confirmed
- Changes where we do not yet have enough validated information to be useful

## Word count
300–600 words

## Required sections

1. **What has changed** (1-2 sentences) — the core fact, immediately
2. **When it takes effect** — effective date
3. **Who it affects** — which ICP segments / client types
4. **What it means in practice** — implications for people going through this process
5. **What action (if any) is required** — what should someone do in response?
6. **Source** — link to the official announcement or legislation
7. **Related guidance** — link to the updated process explainer or guide (if published)
8. **Disclaimer** — accuracy disclaimer with note that we are monitoring developments

## Rules

**Speed matters more than depth.** A news update is not a comprehensive guide. It is a "here is what we know right now" notice. Depth comes later in updated process explainers.

**Only publish what is confirmed.** Do not publish speculation. If the change is announced but details are unclear, say so explicitly.

**Update or replace — do not leave stale news.** Once a news update has been superseded by a full process explainer update, either update the news post to redirect to the new content, or retire it.

**Source every claim.** Link to the official announcement. Do not publish a news update without a traceable source.

## SEO

News updates are indexed but not a primary SEO investment. Focus on the correct factual framing rather than keyword optimisation. The long-term SEO value comes from updating the related process explainer — not from the news update itself.

## Process

1. Identify the change (from news monitoring, partner alert, or official announcement)
2. Verify from the official source
3. Update the related process doc in GitHub (set status to `needs-revalidation`)
4. Publish the news update
5. Create a task in the Content Pipeline to update the full process explainer within 2 weeks
