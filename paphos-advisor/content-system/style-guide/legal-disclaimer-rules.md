# Legal Disclaimer Rules

## Purpose
Defines when, where, and how legal disclaimers must be included in published content. These rules protect the business from liability and ensure clients understand the limits of published information.

---

## Core Principle

We provide information, not legal or financial advice. Our content is educational. Clients who need professional advice (legal, tax, financial) must be directed to appropriate professionals.

This must be communicated clearly — not buried in a footer, not in a generic "this is for information only" page that nobody reads.

---

## Mandatory Disclaimer — Standard (all content)

This disclaimer must appear on every page of the website:

> *The information on this page is provided for general educational purposes. It reflects our understanding as of the date shown and may not reflect the latest regulatory changes. It does not constitute legal, tax, or financial advice. For advice specific to your situation, please consult a qualified professional.*

**Placement:** Footer of every page. Can also appear as a callout box at the top of process-heavy content.

---

## Mandatory Disclaimer — Enhanced (medium/low confidence content)

Where the source material has medium or low confidence, add the enhanced disclaimer in a visible callout box:

> ⚠ **Accuracy note:** The information in this section is based on practitioner experience and may not reflect the current official guidance. Regulations and procedures change frequently. We recommend verifying this information with an immigration lawyer / tax advisor / the relevant authority before acting on it.

**When to use:**
- Any section derived from field sources only (no official corroboration)
- Any process that has had recent known changes that have not yet been fully validated
- Any information with `confidence: low` or `confidence: unverified` in the source process doc

---

## Mandatory Disclaimer — Regulatory Change Notice

When a regulatory change has been announced but not yet fully validated in our system:

> 📋 **Recent change:** This area has been subject to recent regulatory changes. Our information is being updated. Please verify the current position with an official source or professional advisor before acting.

---

## What We Never Say

The following phrases imply legal or financial advice and must not appear in published content:

- "You should..."  (use "In this situation, many people choose to...")
- "You are required to..." (use "The [authority] requires..." or "This process requires...")
- "You will be entitled to..." (use "Under this route, applicants are typically entitled to...")
- "This means you will pay X in tax" (use "Under this structure, the applicable rate is X")
- "This is the best option for you" (use "This route is typically chosen by people who...")

---

## Partner Referral Disclosure

When referring to or recommending a partner:

> *Paphos Advisors works with a network of vetted local professionals. Where we refer you to a third party, we may have a referral arrangement with them. We only refer to professionals we trust, but we encourage you to conduct your own due diligence before engaging any third party.*

This must appear on any page that includes partner profiles or referral callouts.

---

## Review

Legal disclaimer language must be reviewed annually or whenever relevant Cyprus legislation changes. Any change to disclaimer language requires sign-off from Jason before publication.
