# Formatting Standards

## Purpose
Defines visual and structural formatting standards for all published content to ensure a consistent, readable experience.

---

## Page Structure

### Standard content page structure
1. H1 — Page title
2. Opening paragraph (no heading) — answers the key question immediately
3. Last updated notice (for time-sensitive content)
4. Table of contents (for pages > 1,500 words)
5. Body content (H2 → H3 sections)
6. Summary / Key takeaways
7. Next steps or CTA
8. Accuracy disclaimer callout
9. Related content links

---

## Text Formatting

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| H1 | 50-65 characters. One per page. Same as or close to meta title. |
| H2 | Descriptive, not clever. Should make sense out of context. |
| H3 | Subordinate to H2. Max 2 levels of nesting in most content. |
| Paragraph | Max 4 lines on mobile (approx. 60 words). One idea per paragraph. |
| Bold | Key terms, important actions, critical warnings. Not for decoration. |
| Italics | Titles of documents, foreign terms. Minimal use. |
| Bullet list | 2-8 items. Full sentence or consistent noun phrase format within each list. |
| Numbered list | Sequential steps only. |

---

## Tables

Use tables for:
- Document requirement lists (Document | Format | Notes)
- Timeline summaries (Stage | Timeframe | Who)
- Cost summaries (Item | Estimated cost | Notes)
- Comparison of options (Option | Pros | Cons | Best for)

Table headers must be clear labels, not questions. Column widths should be appropriate to the content — do not create very narrow "Notes" columns.

---

## Callout Boxes

Four types of callout box:

| Type | Emoji/icon | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | ⚠ Warning: | Incorrect actions that cause problems, hard deadlines |
| Tip | 💡 Tip: | Practical shortcuts or non-obvious helpful information |
| Accuracy note | 📋 Note: | Confidence caveats, areas of uncertainty |
| Update notice | 🔄 Updated: | Recent changes to the guidance on this page |

---

## Images and Visuals

- Alt text is mandatory for all images. Describe what the image shows.
- No stock photos of Cyprus sunsets. Use process diagrams, screenshots of official forms, or annotated document examples.
- Diagrams should have a text alternative (the same information in a table or list) for accessibility.

---

## Checklists

For checklist content:
- Use checkbox format: `☐ Action verb — specific task`
- Group by phase or stage
- Include a "Documents to Prepare" table
- Include a timeline estimate
- Each checklist should be available as a printable PDF with the version date

PDF naming: `[process-slug]-checklist-[YYYY-MM].pdf`

---

## Process Step Format

When documenting a step-by-step process:

```
### Step [N]: [Action verb + what]

**What you need:** [Documents or prerequisites]

**How to do it:** [Clear instructions]

**Where:** [Location or URL]

**Processing time:** [Official time] / [Real-world time if different]

**Cost:** [Fee if applicable]

**Common issues:** [What goes wrong and how to avoid it]
```

---

## Dates and Freshness

- Include a "Last updated: Month YYYY" notice at the top of all process content
- For regulatory content: include the date of the regulation being referenced ("As of [date]...")
- Do not use relative dates ("last year", "recently") — they become inaccurate quickly
