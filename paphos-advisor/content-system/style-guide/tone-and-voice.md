# Tone and Voice

## Brand Voice Summary

**Knowledgeable, direct, and honest.**

We write like a trusted advisor who has been through the process themselves. We are not a law firm being careful with every word. We are not a lifestyle blog being cheerful. We are the person who tells you exactly what to expect, what the pitfalls are, and what to do about them.

---

## Core Attributes

### 1. Knowledgeable
We know Cyprus. We write with authority based on field intelligence and validated research. We do not hedge on things we know. When we do not know something, we say so clearly.

✓ "The Paphos CRMD office typically processes MEU1 applications in 1-3 months."
✗ "Processing times may vary and could potentially be around a few months or more."

### 2. Direct
We respect the reader's time. We lead with the answer, not the preamble. We do not pad. We use short sentences and plain English.

✓ "You need a stamped rental agreement. This means stamped by the Tax Department, not just signed by the landlord."
✗ "When it comes to the documentation requirements for this particular application, one of the important things to bear in mind is that..."

### 3. Honest
We do not oversell Cyprus. We do not hide complexity or bad news. If a process is slow, we say it is slow. If the rules have grey areas, we say there are grey areas. If we are uncertain, we label it as uncertain.

✓ "There is no published minimum income for the Yellow Slip, but in practice officers expect to see €1,000–1,500/month."
✗ "There are no income requirements for the Yellow Slip." (factually misleading)

### 4. Warm but professional
We write for adults navigating a real life decision. We are not robotic and we are not casual. Friendly, not flippant. Supportive, not patronising.

✓ "Relocating to a new country is complex. We have broken this down into the parts that actually matter."
✗ "Don't worry, it's actually super easy once you know what to do!" (dismissive of genuine complexity)

---

## Things to Avoid

**Jargon without explanation.** If you use a term like "MEU1" or "Non-Dom status", explain it briefly the first time.

**Passive voice overuse.** "Applications must be submitted" → "You must submit your application."

**Vague hedging.** "It may potentially be possible that in some cases..." → say what you know and note your confidence level.

**Promotional language.** We do not write about ourselves in content. Content is not advertising.

**Excessive disclaimers.** One clear disclaimer per page where needed. Not a paragraph of legalese before every paragraph of content.

---

## Reading Level

Write for a well-educated non-expert. The reader is intelligent and capable of understanding complex information — but they are not a lawyer or an accountant. Explain things clearly without dumbing down.

Target: Grade 9-10 reading level (Flesch-Kincaid).

---

## Person and Tense

- **Second person (you/your):** Standard for all instructional content — guides, process explainers, checklists, FAQs
- **Third person:** For describing our company on About/partner pages
- **Present tense:** Default. "The CRMD requires..." not "The CRMD will require..."
- **Active voice:** Default. "Submit the form" not "The form should be submitted."
