# Content Strategy

## Mission

To be the most trusted, accurate, and practically useful source of information for people relocating to Cyprus — particularly to the Paphos area.

We compete on:
- **Accuracy** — field-validated, up-to-date information others do not have
- **Specificity** — Paphos-focused, ICP-specific, process-level detail
- **Trust** — transparent about confidence levels, clear about what we do not know

---

## Strategic Goals

1. **Build topical authority** — dominate the search landscape for Cyprus relocation topics through a comprehensive pillar-cluster content structure
2. **Convert organic traffic** — use content to attract the right ICP segments and convert them to enquiries
3. **Demonstrate expertise** — content that is clearly written by people who know Cyprus processes from the inside, not scraped from government websites

---

## Content Principles

**Accuracy first.** We do not publish content we are not confident is correct. If we are uncertain, we say so explicitly. We would rather publish nothing than publish something misleading.

**ICP-driven.** Every piece of content is written for a specific client segment. Generic content does not serve anyone well.

**Process-grounded.** Every factual claim in our content is traceable to a validated process doc or KB article. We do not write from memory.

**Sustainable.** We publish what we can maintain. A published page we cannot update is a liability. We prefer fewer, well-maintained pieces over many stale ones.

**Non-promotional.** Our content educates and informs. It does not sell. Trust is built by giving genuinely useful information, not by writing advertorials.

## Topical Structure

We use a pillar-cluster model for topical authority. See `content-system/topical-map/` for the full structure.

**6 pillars:**
1. Immigration & Residency
2. Tax & Financial Planning
3. Property (Buying & Renting)
4. Business Formation
5. Healthcare (GESY & Private)
6. Settling In (Banking, Utilities, Schools)

Each pillar has a comprehensive pillar page and multiple supporting cluster pages. All cluster pages link back to the pillar page. Internal links reinforce the topical structure.

---

## Content Calendar Approach

We do not maintain a rigid editorial calendar. Instead, we prioritise by:

1. **Search demand** — what are people searching for that we do not yet cover?
2. **ICP need** — what do our highest-value ICP segments need most?
3. **Knowledge readiness** — do we have validated source material to write from?
4. **Revenue proximity** — what content is closest to the point of enquiry?

The Content Pipeline in Notion is the working queue. Priority is set there.
