# Audience Needs Mapping

## Purpose
Maps each ICP segment to their primary content needs, the questions they are asking, and the content that best serves them. Used to prioritise the Content Pipeline.

---

## EU National — Remote Worker

**Situation:** Working remotely for an EU/EEA employer, looking to base themselves in Cyprus for lifestyle and tax reasons.

**Primary needs:**
- How to register legally (Yellow Slip)
- How to establish Cyprus tax residency (60-Day Rule)
- How to benefit from Non-Dom status
- Where to rent a property that qualifies for MEU1
- How GESY works for employed vs self-employed

**Questions they are asking:**
- "How do I get a Yellow Slip in Cyprus?"
- "What is the 60-day rule for Cyprus tax residency?"
- "Can I get Non-Dom status as an EU national?"
- "Do I need GESY if I already have private insurance?"
- "What documents do I need to rent in Cyprus?"

**Best content for this segment:** Process explainers, step-by-step guides, checklist (documents needed for Yellow Slip), FAQ page on tax residency

---

## UK National — Retiree

**Situation:** Post-Brexit UK national, often retired or on pension income, seeking warm climate and potential tax efficiency.

**Primary needs:**
- Understanding their post-Brexit residency status (not EU, treated as non-EU)
- Category F permit process (the main route for non-working UK nationals)
- Healthcare options (private insurance + GESY)
- Property purchase or rental
- Pension income and Cyprus tax implications

**Questions they are asking:**
- "Can British citizens still move to Cyprus after Brexit?"
- "What visa do British retirees need to live in Cyprus?"
- "Is my UK pension taxed in Cyprus?"
- "Can I buy property in Cyprus as a UK citizen?"
- "How much does it cost to live in Paphos?"

**Best content for this segment:** Category F permit guide, UK pension tax guide, property buyer's guide, cost of living overview

---

## Non-EU Digital Nomad

**Situation:** Non-EU/EEA national working remotely for companies outside Cyprus. Seeking legal residency for 1+ years.

**Primary needs:**
- Digital Nomad Visa eligibility and process
- Tax implications (is Cyprus tax residency beneficial?)
- Accommodation that meets visa requirements
- GESY / health insurance requirements for the visa

**Questions they are asking:**
- "Does Cyprus have a digital nomad visa?"
- "How do I qualify for the Cyprus digital nomad visa?"
- "What income do I need for the Cyprus digital nomad visa?"
- "Can I bring my family on the digital nomad visa?"

**Best content for this segment:** Digital Nomad Visa explainer, FAQ page, comparison with other EU digital nomad visa destinations

---

## Entrepreneur — Company Formation

**Situation:** Founder or business owner seeking to incorporate a Cyprus company for tax efficiency, IP holding, or EU market access.

**Primary needs:**
- Cyprus HE company formation process
- Corporate tax rate and IP Box regime
- Director residency requirements
- VAT registration
- Payroll and social insurance

**Questions they are asking:**
- "How do I set up a company in Cyprus?"
- "What is the corporate tax rate in Cyprus?"
- "What is the Cyprus IP Box regime?"
- "Do I need to live in Cyprus to own a Cyprus company?"

**Best content for this segment:** Company formation guide, IP Box explainer, corporate tax overview, comparison of Cyprus vs Malta vs Ireland for company formation

---

## HNI — Permanent Residency

**Situation:** High net worth individual seeking permanent residency through the Category 6.2 fast-track route (property investment of €300k+).

**Primary needs:**
- Category 6.2 permanent residency requirements
- Qualifying property types and developers
- Due diligence on property investment
- Tax planning (Non-Dom status for dividend income)
- Family inclusion

**Questions they are asking:**
- "How do I get permanent residency in Cyprus?"
- "What is the €300,000 property requirement?"
- "Can my family get permanent residency with me?"
- "How long does Cyprus permanent residency take?"

**Best content for this segment:** Permanent residency guide, property investment checklist, Non-Dom status guide, FAQ page

---

## Family Relocating with Children

**Situation:** Family unit (couple + children) making a full relocation. Multiple processes running in parallel — parents need residency, children need schools.

**Primary needs:**
- Residency for both parents (may be different permit types)
- School registration
- Healthcare for children (GESY)
- Property: need enough space, right school catchment
- Settling in fully — bank accounts, utilities, driving licences

**Questions they are asking:**
- "How do I register my children for school in Cyprus?"
- "What are the best areas in Paphos for families?"
- "Can I add my children to my Yellow Slip?"
- "What vaccinations do children need for Cyprus schools?"
- "How long does it take to get the Yellow Slip for the whole family?"

**Best content for this segment:** Family relocation checklist, school registration guide, family-friendly area guide, comprehensive settling-in guide
