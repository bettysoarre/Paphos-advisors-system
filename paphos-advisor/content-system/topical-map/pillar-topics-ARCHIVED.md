# Topical Map — Pillar Topics

This document defines the six content pillars for Paphos Advisors. Each pillar represents a major topic cluster that we intend to own with topical authority. Supporting content ideas listed under each pillar will become individual Content Pipeline entries in Notion.

**How this map works:**
- Each pillar has one master guide (the "hub") that links to all supporting content
- Supporting content ("spokes") links back to the pillar and to each other where relevant
- Together, the pillar + spokes form a topic cluster that signals authority to search engines and serves readers at every stage of their journey
- Content Pipeline IDs are assigned when a piece moves from idea to briefed

---

## Pillar 1: Moving to Cyprus

**Type:** Master pillar — the overarching guide that sits above all other pillars
**Primary keyword:** moving to Cyprus
**Search intent:** Informational — top of funnel, broadest audience
**Target ICP:** All segments — this is the entry point for every relocator regardless of nationality or purpose

### What This Pillar Covers
The complete journey of relocating to Cyprus, from the initial decision through to being settled. This pillar does not go deep on any single topic — it serves as the map that directs readers to the right sub-pillar or guide for their specific situation. It must answer: is Cyprus right for me, where do I start, and what does the journey actually look like?

### Supporting Content Ideas

1. **Moving to Cyprus: The Complete Guide for [Year]**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   The master hub page. Covers: why Cyprus, who moves there, the key processes in sequence, costs, timeline, and links to all six sub-pillars. 3,000–4,000 words.

2. **Cost of Moving to Cyprus: What to Budget in [Year]**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Comprehensive breakdown of relocation costs: shipping, legal fees, deposits, government fees, setup costs, and ongoing cost of living. Highly shareable and lead-magnet candidate.

3. **Cyprus vs Portugal vs Malta: Which is Right for You?**
   *Type: Comparison | Intent: Commercial | ICP: All*
   Side-by-side comparison across: tax efficiency, cost of living, immigration complexity, property market, lifestyle. Targets readers in the decision phase.

4. **Best Areas to Live in Cyprus: Paphos vs Limassol vs Nicosia**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   District-by-district breakdown for relocators. Covers: expat community, cost of living, property prices, infrastructure, lifestyle. Positions Paphos as the recommendation.

5. **Moving to Cyprus Checklist: Everything You Need to Do Before and After Arrival**
   *Type: Checklist | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Master pre-arrival and post-arrival checklist. High lead magnet potential as a downloadable PDF.

6. **Timeline for Moving to Cyprus: What Happens and When**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Month-by-month or phase-by-phase breakdown of what a typical relocation involves from decision to fully settled. Reduces anxiety and sets expectations.

7. **Moving to Cyprus with a Family: Schools, Healthcare, and Settling In**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Families*
   Dedicated guide for families with children covering: international schools, GESY registration, settling-in for children, and family-friendly areas.

8. **Moving to Cyprus from the UK: A Post-Brexit Guide**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: UK Nationals*
   UK-specific entry point covering: visa routes post-Brexit, banking, driving licences, pension implications, and the UK-Cyprus double tax treaty.

## Pillar 2: Cyprus Immigration & Residency

**Type:** Sub-pillar
**Primary keyword:** Cyprus residency for expats
**Search intent:** Informational to commercial — readers are actively researching their immigration route
**Target ICP:** EU nationals, UK nationals, third-country nationals, digital nomads, retirees

### What This Pillar Covers
Every immigration and residency route available in Cyprus — who qualifies for each, what the process involves, and how to choose the right one. This pillar sits directly below Pillar 1 and is the most process-heavy cluster in the system.

### Supporting Content Ideas

1. **Cyprus Residency Options: Which Route is Right for You?**
   *Type: Comparison | Intent: Commercial | ICP: All*
   Overview of all residency routes (MEU1, Pink Slip/Category F, Digital Nomad Visa, work permit, self-employed) with a decision-tree style guide to help readers identify their route. Hub page for this pillar.

2. **Cyprus Yellow Slip Guide: How EU Nationals Register Their Residency (MEU1)**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: EU Nationals*
   Full guide based on PROC-IMM-001. Most searched immigration topic for EU nationals. Includes documents, fees, timeline, district comparison.

3. **Moving to Cyprus from the UK After Brexit: Your Residency Options**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: UK Nationals*
   UK-specific immigration guide covering Category F (financially independent), work permits, and the Pink Slip legacy situation. High search demand.

4. **Cyprus Digital Nomad Visa: How to Apply and Who Qualifies**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Digital Nomads*
   Complete guide to the Cyprus Digital Nomad Visa — eligibility, income requirements, application process, and what it leads to.

5. **Cyprus Category F Visa: The Financially Independent Person Route**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Retirees, UK Nationals, Third-Country Nationals*
   Guide to Cyprus's Category F permit for non-working residents with sufficient income. Popular among retirees and those with pension income.

6. **Bringing Your Family to Cyprus: MEU2 and Dependent Permits**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Families, All*
   Guide to registering non-EU family members and dependents alongside an EU national primary applicant.

7. **Cyprus Work Permit Guide for Non-EU Nationals**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Third-Country Nationals*
   Step-by-step guide to the Cyprus work permit process for third-country nationals sponsored by a Cyprus employer.

8. **How Long Can You Stay in Cyprus Without Registering?**
   *Type: FAQ cluster | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Addresses the 3-month rule, the 90/180-day Schengen question (Cyprus is not Schengen), and the implications of staying without registering.

9. **Cyprus Residency FAQs: Your Most Common Questions Answered**
   *Type: FAQ cluster | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   10–15 FAQ entries grouped under immigration — what documents do I need, how long does it take, can my partner come, etc.

## Pillar 3: Cyprus Tax for Expats

**Type:** Sub-pillar
**Primary keyword:** Cyprus tax for expats
**Search intent:** Informational to commercial — high-intent readers with money at stake
**Target ICP:** Business owners, digital nomads, retirees, UK nationals, anyone with investment income

### What This Pillar Covers
The full Cyprus tax picture for relocating individuals — residency rules, the non-dom regime, income tax rates, double tax treaties, annual filing obligations, and the interaction between Cyprus tax residency and foreign tax obligations.

### Supporting Content Ideas

1. **Cyprus Tax for Expats: The Complete Guide for [Year]**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners, Digital Nomads, Retirees*
   Hub page for the tax pillar. Covers: income tax rates, non-dom overview, 60-day rule, double tax treaties, GHS contributions, and what needs to be filed. 3,000+ words.

2. **The Cyprus 60-Day Tax Residency Rule Explained**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners, Digital Nomads, Multi-step Relocators*
   Full guide based on PROC-TAX-PROC-001. Covers all four qualifying conditions, the 2026 rule change, and how to structure your year to qualify.

3. **Cyprus Non-Dom Status: How to Pay 0% Tax on Dividends**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Commercial | ICP: Business Owners, Investors*
   Full guide based on PROC-TAX-CONF-001. Targets high-intent searchers — business owners and investors actively looking for dividend tax optimisation. High-value lead generation.

4. **Cyprus Income Tax Rates and Bands for [Year]**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Clean, factual guide to Cyprus personal income tax — rates, bands, allowances, and how employment income is taxed. High search volume, good anchor for internal linking.

5. **Cyprus vs UK Taxation: What Changes When You Relocate**
   *Type: Comparison | Intent: Commercial | ICP: UK Nationals*
   Side-by-side comparison of UK and Cyprus tax treatment for income, dividends, capital gains, and pensions. Highly relevant for UK nationals considering relocation.

6. **The UK-Cyprus Double Tax Treaty Explained**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: UK Nationals*
   Explains what the treaty covers, how it prevents double taxation on income and pensions, and the implications for UK nationals living in Cyprus.

7. **How to File Your Tax Return in Cyprus: A Step-by-Step Guide**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All Tax Residents*
   Practical guide to the annual tax return process — registration on Taxisnet, what to declare, deadlines, and what to do if you use an accountant.

8. **Cyprus Tax FAQs: What Expats Ask Most**
   *Type: FAQ cluster | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   10–15 FAQ entries covering: do I need to file, what is the non-dom deadline, can I keep UK tax residency, how does GHS work, etc.

9. **Special Defence Contribution (SDC) in Cyprus: What Expats Need to Know**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners, Investors*
   Focused guide on SDC — what it is, who pays it, the 2026 reform, and how non-dom status changes your exposure.

## Pillar 4: Buying Property in Cyprus

**Type:** Sub-pillar
**Primary keyword:** buying property in Cyprus
**Search intent:** Informational to commercial — mix of researchers and active buyers
**Target ICP:** Property buyers, retirees, business owners, multi-step relocators

### What This Pillar Covers
The complete property purchase journey in Cyprus — from finding a property through to title deed transfer. Covers costs, legal requirements, the title deed problem, mortgage access, and the practical realities of the market.

### Supporting Content Ideas

1. **Buying Property in Cyprus: The Complete Guide for Foreigners**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Property Buyers, All*
   Hub page for the property pillar. Covers the full purchase process, key costs, legal requirements, title deed situation, and what to watch out for. 3,000+ words.

2. **How to Buy Property in Cyprus: Step-by-Step Process**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Property Buyers*
   Detailed step-by-step guide to the purchase process from initial search through to title deed transfer. Based on the PROC-PROP process documents when built.

3. **Costs of Buying Property in Cyprus: Taxes, Fees, and Hidden Costs**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Property Buyers*
   Complete cost breakdown: transfer fees, VAT on new builds, stamp duty (abolished 2026), legal fees, agent commission, and ongoing annual costs (municipality tax, GHS). High search demand.

4. **Cyprus Title Deeds: The Problem and How to Protect Yourself**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Property Buyers*
   Explains the infamous Cyprus title deed backlog — why some properties have no title deed, what the risks are, and how to protect yourself as a buyer. A trust-building piece that shows depth of knowledge.

5. **New Build vs Resale Property in Cyprus: Which is Better?**
   *Type: Comparison | Intent: Commercial | ICP: Property Buyers*
   Comparison of buying off-plan/new build versus existing resale property — VAT implications, title deed timing, risk profile, and lifestyle considerations.

6. **Can Foreigners Buy Property in Cyprus? Rules for Non-EU Nationals**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Third-Country Nationals, UK Nationals*
   Addresses the specific rules and Council of Ministers approval requirements for non-EU nationals purchasing property.

7. **Getting a Mortgage in Cyprus as a Foreigner**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Property Buyers*
   How Cyprus mortgages work for non-residents and new residents — eligibility, LTV ratios, interest rates, documentation, and the main lenders.

8. **Best Areas to Buy Property in Paphos: A Neighbourhood Guide**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Commercial | ICP: Property Buyers*
   Area-by-area guide to Paphos districts — Kato Paphos, Peyia, Chloraka, Tala, Coral Bay — covering price points, character, and what type of buyer suits each area.

9. **Property Buying FAQs for Foreigners in Cyprus**
   *Type: FAQ cluster | Intent: Informational | ICP: Property Buyers*
   10–12 FAQ entries: do I need a lawyer, what is the title deed situation, can I rent it out, how long does it take, etc.

## Pillar 5: Setting Up a Business in Cyprus

**Type:** Sub-pillar
**Primary keyword:** setting up a business in Cyprus
**Search intent:** Commercial — readers actively considering Cyprus for business
**Target ICP:** Business owners, digital nomads with income structure needs, multi-step relocators

### What This Pillar Covers
Company formation, the Cyprus corporate tax environment, ongoing compliance requirements, and the interaction between business structure and personal tax planning (particularly non-dom status). This pillar serves business owners who want to understand both the setup process and the tax efficiency case.

### Supporting Content Ideas

1. **Setting Up a Business in Cyprus: The Complete Guide**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners*
   Hub page. Covers: why Cyprus for business, company types, formation process, corporate tax rate (12.5%), ongoing compliance, and the link to personal tax planning. 3,000+ words.

2. **How to Form a Company in Cyprus: Step-by-Step**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners*
   Detailed guide to the Cyprus company formation process — choosing a structure, Registrar of Companies, required documents, timeline, and costs.

3. **Cyprus Corporate Tax: What Business Owners Need to Know**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners*
   Covers: 12.5% corporate tax rate, allowable deductions, dividend distribution to non-dom shareholders (0% SDC), and the IP Box regime.

4. **Cyprus LTD vs Sole Trader: Which Structure is Right for You?**
   *Type: Comparison | Intent: Commercial | ICP: Business Owners, Freelancers*
   Comparison of the two most common structures for self-employed individuals and small business owners in Cyprus.

5. **VAT Registration in Cyprus: When You Need It and How to Register**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners*
   VAT thresholds, registration process, intra-EU VAT for service businesses, and ongoing filing obligations.

6. **Opening a Business Bank Account in Cyprus**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners*
   Practical guide to opening a Cyprus corporate bank account — which banks, what documents are required, compliance checks, and realistic timelines.

7. **The Cyprus IP Box Regime: Tax Benefits for Intellectual Property**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Commercial | ICP: Business Owners, Digital Businesses*
   Overview of the Cyprus IP Box — 2.5% effective tax rate on qualifying IP income, what qualifies, and how to structure it.

8. **Hiring Employees in Cyprus: What Employers Need to Know**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners*
   Employment law basics, social insurance contributions, minimum wage, and what is required when hiring EU and non-EU employees.

9. **Cyprus Business Setup FAQs**
   *Type: FAQ cluster | Intent: Informational | ICP: Business Owners*
   10–12 FAQ entries: how long does it take, do I need a local director, can I run a UK company from Cyprus, what is the annual compliance cost, etc.

## Pillar 6: Living in Paphos

**Type:** Sub-pillar — lifestyle and settling-in
**Primary keyword:** living in Paphos
**Search intent:** Informational — readers who have decided to move and want to know what daily life looks like
**Target ICP:** Retirees, families, digital nomads, UK nationals, anyone choosing where to live

### What This Pillar Covers
The practical and lifestyle reality of living in Paphos specifically — healthcare, transport, daily life, schooling, social scene, cost of living, and what to expect in the first year. This pillar converts readers who are past the decision stage and into client conversations about the actual move.

### Supporting Content Ideas

1. **Living in Paphos: The Honest Expat Guide for [Year]**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Hub page. Covers: the character of Paphos, pros and cons, cost of living, expat community, key services, and what to expect in year one. 3,000+ words.

2. **Healthcare in Cyprus for Expats: GESY, Private Insurance, and How It Works**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Complete guide to the Cyprus General Healthcare System (GESY) — who can register, how to register, what is covered, and when private insurance is still needed.

3. **Driving in Cyprus: Importing Your Car, Getting a Cyprus Licence**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Covers: left-hand traffic, importing a vehicle, re-registration, exchanging a foreign driving licence, and road rules expats commonly get wrong.

4. **Cost of Living in Paphos: A Realistic Monthly Budget for Expats**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Detailed monthly cost breakdown — rent, utilities, food, transport, healthcare, dining out — for singles, couples, and families. Updated annually.

5. **Opening a Bank Account in Cyprus as a Foreigner**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Which banks operate in Cyprus, what documents are required, how long it takes, and the challenges non-residents face. Covers Bank of Cyprus, Hellenic Bank, and online alternatives.

6. **International Schools in Paphos: A Guide for Expat Families**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: Families*
   Overview of English-language and international schools in and around Paphos — curriculum, fees, waiting lists, and the admissions process.

7. **Paphos Neighbourhoods: Where to Live as an Expat**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Area guide to Kato Paphos, Paphos town, Chloraka, Peyia, Tala, and surrounding villages — character, rental prices, facilities, and who each area suits.

8. **The First 30 Days in Paphos: Your Settling-In Action Plan**
   *Type: Checklist + Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Combines a narrative guide with a downloadable checklist — what to do in the first 30 days: register at CRMD, open a bank account, register with GESY, sort utilities, get a Cypriot SIM, find a GP.

9. **Utilities in Cyprus: Electricity, Water, Gas, and Internet Setup**
   *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
   Practical guide to setting up EAC electricity, local water board, bottled gas, fibre/broadband, and mobile in Cyprus. Covers costs and process.

10. **What Expats Wish They Knew Before Moving to Paphos**
    *Type: Guide | Intent: Informational | ICP: All*
    Experience-driven piece drawing on common mistakes, surprises, and practical wisdom from people who have already made the move. High shareability and social media potential.
