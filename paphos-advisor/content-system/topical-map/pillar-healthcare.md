## Notes

- GESY contribution rate: 2.65% of gross income (employee portion), capped at €180k
- GESY applies to all registered residents including Non-Dom — a common misconception
- GESY card needed to access public hospitals and GP services — practical guide for new arrivals
- Private insurance is often needed alongside GESY for more comprehensive coverage
- Category F permit applicants must have comprehensive health insurance — private insurance is the route
