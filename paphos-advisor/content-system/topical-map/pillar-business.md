## Notes

- Cyprus has 12.5% corporate tax rate — one of the lowest in the EU
- IP Box allows effective rate of 2.5% on qualifying IP income
- Substance requirements for holding companies are increasingly enforced — this is a nuanced area
- Directors do not need to be Cyprus residents to own a Cyprus company — but tax residence is separate from company residence
- Key audience is founders and entrepreneurs, not just accountants — write for the decision-maker
