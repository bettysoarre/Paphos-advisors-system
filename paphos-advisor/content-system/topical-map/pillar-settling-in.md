## Notes

- Bank account opening is reportedly getting harder for non-EU nationals — this is a known pain point for clients

- Driving licence exchange timeline varies significantly by country of origin — needs clear breakdown by nationality type
- EAC electricity connection requires Yellow Slip or other proof of legal residence — reinforces the residency registration sequence
- Schools in Cyprus have a mixture of English-language private schools and state schools — families want guidance on this
- AR card (Aliens Registration) is a requirement for some non-EU nationals — often confused with Yellow Slip
