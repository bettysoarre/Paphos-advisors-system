## Notes

- Stamp duty abolished January 2026 — open question whether Tax-stamped rental contract still required for MEU1 post-abolition (flagged in PROC-PROP-001 and PROC-IMM-001)
- Title deed situation in Cyprus is complex — many properties have delayed or no title deeds; this is a major risk that must be covered clearly in buyer guides
- Minimum 12-month tenancy for MEU1 qualification — important to note in rental guide
- Non-EU nationals need Council of Ministers approval to buy property — important distinction
