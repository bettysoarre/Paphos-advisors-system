# Segmentation Model

## How Segments Are Defined

ICP segments are defined by the intersection of four dimensions:

1. **Nationality category** — EU/EEA, UK (post-Brexit), or Non-EU. Determines which immigration routes are available.
2. **Life stage / motivation** — Why are they moving? (Retirement, work, business, investment, family)
3. **Primary process cluster** — Which Cyprus processes they will need to navigate
4. **Revenue profile** — What they typically spend with advisors and the complexity of their case

---

## Segment Differentiation

### Nationality determines the immigration route
- EU/EEA nationals → Yellow Slip (MEU1) is the residency foundation
- UK nationals (post-Brexit) → Category F permit is the primary route
- Non-EU nationals → Category F, Digital Nomad Visa, or Category EP depending on situation
- HNI (any nationality) → May pursue fast-track permanent residency (Category 6.2)

### Motivation determines the tax and financial planning priority
- Tax-motivated (remote workers, entrepreneurs, HNIs) → 60-day rule, Non-Dom, corporate structuring
- Lifestyle-motivated (retirees, families) → Less focus on tax planning, more on settling in
- Investment-motivated (property investors) → Property law, capital gains, rental income

### Life stage determines the settling-in needs
- Families → Schools, healthcare, family housing, larger vehicles
- Retirees → Healthcare, pension income, community
- Workers → Fast setup, connectivity, bank account
- Entrepreneurs → Business infrastructure, banking, accounting

## Segment Priority Rationale

**ICP-02 (EU National — Remote Worker) is Priority 1 because:**
- Largest addressable market in our geography
- Multiple process needs (immigration + tax + property) = higher revenue per case
- Strong organic search demand for their specific questions
- Often come with partners/spouses, doubling the value of the relationship

**ICP-01 (UK National — Retiree) is Priority 2 because:**
- Large volume of UK nationals relocating post-Brexit seeking guidance
- High willingness to pay for advisory services
- Often bring significant pension and investment assets
- Healthcare and lifestyle needs create sustained relationship opportunities

**ICP-04 (Entrepreneur) is Priority 3 because:**
- Highest revenue per case (company formation + tax structuring + personal tax)
- Strong referral potential (entrepreneurs know other entrepreneurs)
- Content for this segment differentiates us from basic immigration services

## How to Assign a Segment at Intake

During case intake, assign the primary ICP segment based on:
1. Nationality → narrows the immigration route
2. Employment status → remote worker, employed, self-employed, retired, business owner
3. Primary goal → why are they coming to Cyprus?
4. Family status → individual, couple, family with children

If a client matches multiple segments (common), assign the primary segment based on their dominant need and note the secondary segment in case notes.
