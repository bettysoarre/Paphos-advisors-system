# ICP Framework Overview

## What is an ICP?

An Ideal Client Profile (ICP) is a detailed description of the type of client who gets the most value from our services and provides the most value to our business. ICPs are not just demographics — they are a combination of situation, motivation, process needs, and readiness.

---

## Why ICPs Matter

Without ICP definitions:
- Content is written for "everyone" and resonates with nobody
- Partners are referred without knowing if the client is a match for their expertise
- Services are designed without knowing what clients actually need
- Cases take longer because the advisor has to start from scratch understanding each client's situation

With ICP definitions:
- Content is written for specific people with specific questions
- Referral routing is systematic
- Service packages can be pre-designed for known need clusters
- Onboarding is faster because we know the typical profile before the call

---

## The Paphos Advisors ICP Segments

We have defined 9 ICP segments. These are not exhaustive — real clients are often combinations — but they represent the primary need clusters:

| ID | Segment | Primary driver |
|---|---|---|
| ICP-01 | UK National — Retiree | Lifestyle, pension, healthcare |
| ICP-02 | EU National — Remote Worker | Tax efficiency, lifestyle, flexibility |
| ICP-03 | Non-EU Digital Nomad | Legal residency, tax neutral |
| ICP-04 | Entrepreneur — Company Formation | Corporate tax, IP Box, EU access |
| ICP-05 | HNI — Permanent Residency | Status, wealth structuring, security |
| ICP-06 | Family Relocating with Children | Schools, community, housing |
| ICP-07 | Intra-Company Transfer | Corporate mandate, compliance |
| ICP-08 | Property Investor (Non-Resident) | Investment, capital appreciation |
| ICP-09 | Return Diaspora | Family, heritage, familiarity |

Priority order for content and service design: ICP-02, ICP-01, ICP-04, ICP-03, ICP-05, ICP-06.

---

## How ICPs Are Used

**Content:** Every content brief references one or more target ICPs. Content is written for the specific situation and questions of that ICP.

**Cases:** Every case in Notion is tagged with an ICP segment at intake. This drives routing and allows analysis of which segments generate the most cases over time.

**Partners:** Partner referral routing considers the client's ICP — different ICPs need different partner expertise combinations.

**Service design:** Services and packages can be scoped around ICP need clusters (e.g., an "EU Remote Worker Relocation Package" that bundles Yellow Slip + tax residency advisory + Non-Dom setup).
