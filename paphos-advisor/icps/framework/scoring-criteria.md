# ICP Scoring Criteria

## Purpose
Helps assess how closely a prospective client matches our ideal client profiles, and whether to accept a case. Also used to identify which segment to assign at intake.

---

## Fit Score

Score a prospect on four dimensions (1-3 each). Maximum score: 12.

### Dimension 1: Process Need Alignment
Does the client need processes we know well and can advise on?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 3 | Needs 2+ processes we have validated documentation for |
| 2 | Needs 1 process we have validated documentation for |
| 1 | Needs processes we have not yet documented or validated |

### Dimension 2: ICP Segment Match
How closely does this client match a defined ICP segment?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 3 | Clear match to a Priority 1-3 ICP segment |
| 2 | Match to a lower-priority ICP segment |
| 1 | Does not clearly match any defined segment (edge case) |

### Dimension 3: Timeline Realism
Is the client's timeline realistic given current processing times?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 3 | Timeline is realistic — they have time to plan properly |
| 2 | Tight but achievable — will require proactive management |
| 1 | Timeline is unrealistic — they need an outcome faster than the process allows |

### Dimension 4: Client Readiness
Is the client prepared to engage properly?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 3 | Informed, realistic, and ready to provide documents/information when needed |
| 2 | Some knowledge gaps but good engagement |
| 1 | Expects us to do everything with minimal input from them, or has unrealistic expectations |

---

## Scoring Guidance

| Total score | Decision |
|---|---|
| 10-12 | Excellent fit — prioritise onboarding |
| 7-9 | Good fit — proceed with appropriate scope setting |
| 4-6 | Marginal fit — proceed only if specific concerns can be addressed at intake |
| 3 | Poor fit — consider declining or redirecting |

---

## Segment Assignment

After scoring, assign the ICP segment based on:

| If the client is... | Assign segment |
|---|---|
| EU/EEA national, working remotely, seeking residency + tax efficiency | ICP-02 EU Remote Worker |
| UK national, retired or approaching retirement, lifestyle-driven | ICP-01 UK Retiree |
| Non-EU national, employed remotely by foreign company | ICP-03 Non-EU Digital Nomad |
| Any nationality, forming a Cyprus company | ICP-04 Entrepreneur |
| High net worth, seeking permanent residency or citizenship | ICP-05 HNI |
| Moving with children, school registration is a priority | ICP-06 Family |
| Transferred by employer to Cyprus role | ICP-07 Intra-Company Transfer |
| Buying Cyprus property as an investment (not primary residence) | ICP-08 Property Investor |
| Cyprus heritage, family ties, returning | ICP-09 Return Diaspora |

Note the segment in the Case record at intake. It does not need to be perfect — update it if the picture becomes clearer during the case.
