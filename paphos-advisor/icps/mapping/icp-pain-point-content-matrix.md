# ICP Pain Point to Content Matrix

Maps each ICP segment's specific pain points to content ideas across three pillars. Use this to prioritise the Content Pipeline and ensure no ICP is underserved.

**Three content pillars:**
- **Method / How-to** — Process guides, step-by-step explainers, eligibility guides. The "how do I do this" content.
- **Housekeeping** — Checklists, document lists, timelines, compliance requirements. The "what do I need / what must I do" content.
- **Lifestyle** — Area guides, cost of living, schools, community, day-to-day life. The "what is it actually like" content.

**Status key:** ✅ Exists | 🔄 In progress | ⬜ Not yet created
