# ICP-02: EU National — Remote Worker

## Profile Summary

A citizen of an EU or EEA member state (commonly French, German, Dutch, Scandinavian, Romanian, or other EU national) who works remotely — either employed by a company outside Cyprus or as a freelancer/contractor. Motivated by a combination of lifestyle, tax efficiency, and flexibility. EU freedom of movement significantly simplifies their residency process.

---

## Demographics

| Attribute | Typical profile |
|---|---|
| Nationality | EU/EEA (any member state except Cyprus) |
| Age range | 28-48 |
| Employment status | Remote employee, freelancer, contractor, or self-employed |
| Income source | Remote employment income, freelance income, consulting fees |
| Family status | Individual, couple, or family with young children |
| Property intention | Renting (12+ months needed for MEU1) |
| Digital literacy | High |

---

## Motivation for Moving

- **Tax efficiency:** Non-Dom status offers exemption from Dividend Defence Contribution (SDC) and can significantly reduce tax on foreign income. The 60-Day Rule allows tax residency without living in Cyprus full-time.
- **Lifestyle:** Climate, outdoors, Mediterranean pace of life, expat community.
- **EU freedom of movement:** Right to live and work in Cyprus without a work permit — simpler than for non-EU nationals.
- **Cost vs quality of life:** Perceived as better value than equivalent Western European cities.

---

## Key Challenges and Fears

- **Process complexity:** Unsure which residency registration they need, what documents are required, and in what order.
- **Tax residency:** Unsure how to establish Cyprus tax residency without giving up their home country connections.
- **Rental market:** Concerned about finding a compliant rental (stamped contract, 12+ months) in time to proceed with MEU1.
- **Bank account:** Opening a bank account as a new resident is reportedly getting harder.
- **Timeline:** Often want everything sorted quickly and are frustrated by processing times.

---

## Processes Required (Typical)

1. **Yellow Slip (MEU1)** — primary residency registration
2. **TIC (Tax Identification Code)** — needed before MEU1 and for tax residency
3. **60-Day Rule Tax Residency Setup** — if seeking tax residency
4. **Non-Dom Status Application** — to benefit from SDC exemption on dividends
5. **GESY Registration** — healthcare access
6. **Bank Account Opening** — essential for daily life
7. **Property rental** — 12+ month lease needed for MEU1

---
