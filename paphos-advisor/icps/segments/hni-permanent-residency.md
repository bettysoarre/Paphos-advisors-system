# ICP-05: HNI — Permanent Residency

## Profile Summary

A high net worth individual (HNI) seeking Cyprus permanent residency through the fast-track Category 6.2 route, which requires a minimum €300,000 investment in new residential property from a developer. Typically, also interested in tax planning (Non-Dom status) and wealth structuring. Often from non-EU countries seeking an EU permanent residency base.

---

## Demographics

| Attribute | Typical profile |
|---|---|
| Nationality | Non-EU (commonly Israeli, Russian, South African, MENA, South/East Asian) |
| Age range | 40-65 |
| Employment status | Business owner, investor, retired |
| Net worth | €2M+ (typically more) |
| Income source | Dividends, investment income, rental income, pension |
| Family status | Often relocating family unit (spouse + children) |
| Property intention | Buying (€300k minimum on qualifying property) |

---

## Motivation for Moving

- **EU permanent residency status:** The primary driver — provides an EU base, travel rights, security
- **Tax efficiency:** Non-Dom status on dividend income; no Cyprus wealth or inheritance tax
- **Asset security:** Cyprus as a stable, EU-governed jurisdiction for asset holding
- **Quality of life:** Climate, safety, English-speaking environment
- **Family security:** Permanent status for entire family unit

---

## Key Challenges and Fears

- **Property qualifying criteria:** Which properties qualify for the €300k threshold? What developer and what type?
- **Due diligence:** Concerned about property quality, developer reputation, and title deed issues
- **Process timeline:** How long does the application take? What status do they have while waiting?
- **Tax structuring:** How to structure dividend income, investments, and assets for optimal efficiency
- **Family inclusion:** How are spouse and dependent children included?
- **Ongoing conditions:** What are the ongoing requirements to maintain permanent residency?

---

## Processes Required (Typical)

1. **Permanent Residency Category 6.2 Application** — core requirement
2. **Property Purchase (€300k minimum, new property from developer)** — prerequisite
3. **Non-Dom Status Application** — tax efficiency on dividend income
4. **Corporate Tax Planning** — if they have Cyprus or offshore company structures
5. **Clean Criminal Record Certificate** — from country of origin
6. **Health Insurance** — private health insurance is required
