# ICP-06: Family Relocating with Children

## Profile Summary

A family unit — typically two adults with one or more school-age children — making a full relocation to Cyprus. Often the adults fall into other ICP segments (e.g., one EU remote worker + one UK retiree/partner) but the family dimension adds specific needs around schools, healthcare for children, suitable housing, and community. The children's needs create a distinct process layer.

---

## Demographics

| Attribute | Typical profile |
|---|---|
| Nationality | EU, UK, or non-EU (one or both parents) |
| Age range (adults) | 35-50 |
| Children | 1-3 children, typically aged 5-16 |
| Employment status | At least one parent working remotely or employed locally |
| Income source | Employment, freelance, or business income |
| Property intention | Renting (initially), buying (often within 1-2 years) |
| Housing need | 3+ bedrooms, near good schools, family-friendly area |

---

## Motivation for Moving

- **Lifestyle and safety:** Cyprus is safe, family-friendly, English-speaking
- **Schools:** International and private English-medium schools available
- **Outdoor lifestyle:** Year-round outdoor activity, Mediterranean environment for children
- **One or both parents' work situation:** Remote work enables the move
- **Tax efficiency (secondary):** Less primary for this segment, but still a consideration

---

## Key Challenges and Fears

- **Schools:** What schools are available? How do they get in? What are the costs? Are they good?
- **Healthcare for children:** How does GESY work for children? What vaccinations are needed?
- **Residency for both parents:** If parents have different nationalities (EU + UK, for example), they may be on different permit routes
- **Housing:** Finding large enough, affordable housing in the right school catchment area
- **Community:** Will their children make friends? Are there other expat children?
- **Logistics of the move:** Shipping, costs, timing around school year

---

## Processes Required (Typical)

**For each parent — dependent on nationality:**
- EU parent: Yellow Slip (MEU1)
- UK parent: Category F Permit
- Children: Added to parent's registration in most cases

**Additional processes:**
1. **School Registration** — state school (free) or private/international school
2. **GESY Registration** — for all family members
3. **Property Rental** — family-sized, near schools
4. **Bank Account** — joint or individual
5. **Driving Licence Exchange** — if relocating vehicles
6. **Vehicle Import** — if bringing a family car

---

## Partner Types Needed

- Immigration lawyer (each parent's permit, family inclusion)
- Property agent (family housing, school catchment area)
- Healthcare provider (GESY registration for children, family GP)
- Removal company (typically shipping significant household goods)
