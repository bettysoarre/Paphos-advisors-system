# ICP-03: Non-EU Digital Nomad

## Profile Summary

A citizen of a non-EU/EEA country (commonly US, Canadian, Australian, South African, or from MENA/Asian markets) who works remotely for a company or clients based outside Cyprus. Seeking legal residency in Cyprus for 12+ months, typically through the Digital Nomad Visa route.

---

## Demographics

| Attribute | Typical profile |
|---|---|
| Nationality | Non-EU/EEA (US, Canada, Australia, South Africa, Israel, etc.) |
| Age range | 25-40 |
| Employment status | Remote employee or freelancer working for non-Cyprus clients/employers |
| Income source | Foreign employment income, freelance fees |
| Family status | Often individual or couple without children |
| Property intention | Renting |
| Digital literacy | Very high |

---

## Motivation for Moving

- **EU base:** Cyprus is an EU country — useful for travel and as a stepping stone
- **Lifestyle:** Mediterranean climate, English-speaking, growing digital nomad community
- **Visa clarity:** The Digital Nomad Visa provides a defined legal pathway (vs some countries where the status is grey)
- **Tax neutrality:** Less tax-motivated than EU remote workers since Non-Dom benefits are less compelling for those without significant dividend income

---

## Key Challenges and Fears

- **Visa eligibility:** Unsure if they qualify (income requirement, employer type)
- **Health insurance:** Private health insurance is required for the Digital Nomad Visa
- **Complexity vs other destinations:** Cyprus competes with other Digital Nomad Visa destinations (Portugal, Spain, Georgia, Thailand)
- **What happens after the visa expires?** — uncertain about longer-term options

---

## Processes Required (Typical)

1. **Digital Nomad Visa** — primary residency route
2. **Private Health Insurance** — required for the visa application
3. **Property Rental** — proof of accommodation needed
4. **Bank Account** — complex for non-EU nationals; some use fintech solutions initially
5. **TIC** — if they decide to pursue Cyprus tax residency

---

## Partner Types Needed

- Healthcare provider (private health insurance for visa)
- Property agent (rental accommodation)
