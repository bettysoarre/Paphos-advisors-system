# ICP-04: Entrepreneur — Company Formation

## Profile Summary

A founder, business owner, or senior executive who is incorporating a Cyprus company — primarily for corporate tax efficiency (12.5% rate), IP Box benefits, or as an EU holding structure. Often, but not always, also relocating personally to Cyprus to establish tax residency alongside the company.

---

## Demographics

| Attribute | Typical profile |
|---|---|
| Nationality | Any (EU, UK, non-EU) — most common: UK, Israeli, Russian, Eastern European |
| Age range | 30-55 |
| Employment status | Business owner, founder, director |
| Income source | Dividends, management fees, salary from own company |
| Business type | Tech/SaaS, consulting, professional services, holding/IP companies |
| Family status | Varies — often relocating as an individual or couple initially |
| Tax motivation | Very high — this is the primary driver |

---

## Motivation for Moving

- **Corporate tax rate:** 12.5% is one of the lowest in the EU
- **IP Box:** Effective 2.5% rate on qualifying IP income — highly attractive for tech companies
- **Non-Dom status:** 0% tax on dividends received from abroad for qualifying Non-Dom residents
- **EU passport for company:** Cyprus HE company has EU status, useful for market access
- **Substance:** Cyprus has fewer substance requirements than some other jurisdictions

---

## Key Challenges and Fears

- **Substance requirements:** Increasing scrutiny of Cyprus companies without genuine economic substance
- **Complexity:** Company formation + personal tax residency + Non-Dom is a multi-part process — needs coordinated advice
- **Time:** Entrepreneurs typically want speed — they are setting up for a specific date
- **Director residency:** Understanding whether directors need to be resident in Cyprus (they don't to own a company, but it affects where the company is managed and controlled)
- **Banking:** Corporate banking in Cyprus is notoriously difficult to set up for new companies

---

## Processes Required (Typical)

1. **HE Company Formation** — core requirement
2. **Personal Yellow Slip or Permit** — if personally relocating (EU = MEU1, UK/Non-EU = Category F or DN Visa)
3. **60-Day Rule Tax Residency** — to establish personal Cyprus tax residency
4. **Non-Dom Status** — to benefit from SDC exemption on dividends
5. **VAT Registration** — if turnover exceeds threshold or for intra-EU transactions
6. **Payroll / Social Insurance** — if employing staff
7. **IP Box Setup** — if qualifying IP income
