# Third-Party Sources (RES-SRC-002)

## Purpose
Catalogues the third-party sources that may be used to supplement official sources, defines which are acceptable and at what confidence level, and lists sources that must not be used.

---

## Tier 2 — Acceptable Supplementary Sources

These sources may be used to corroborate or contextualise official guidance, but must not serve as the primary basis for any claim. Confidence output: `medium` at best.

### Professional Bodies

| Organisation | Domain | Why acceptable | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICPAC | Tax, accounting | Professional body; member accountability | Use for interpretation, not primary rules |
| Cyprus Bar Association | Legal, immigration | Professional body; member accountability | Use for practice guidance, not legal authority |
| CREAA | Property | Regulatory body for agents | Use for agent registration verification |
| CySEC | Financial services | Regulatory authority | Use for regulated financial advice boundaries |

### EU and International Bodies

| Organisation | Domain | Why acceptable | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| OECD | Business, tax (BEPS) | International standard-setter | Use for substance requirement context |
| European Parliament | EU law | Legislative authority | For EU directive interpretation |
| EUR-Lex | EU law | Official EU legislation database | For EU regulations applicable in Cyprus |

### Reputable Legal and Advisory Publications

These are acceptable for contextual intelligence and interpretation, not for primary facts:

| Publication | Domain | Standard required |
|---|---|---|
| Named partner firm publications (e.g., Kinanis, Patrikios Pavlou) | Immigration, tax, property, business | Named author required; publication date within 12 months |
| KPMG Cyprus / Deloitte Cyprus / EY Cyprus | Tax, business | Named publication; within 12 months of budget they reference |
| Chambers and Partners Cyprus | Legal | For partner landscape context only |

**Requirement:** Named author, firm name, publication date clearly visible. Anonymous firm publications are not acceptable.

---

## Tier 3 — Lower-Confidence Sources (use with caution)

These sources may surface useful leads or community intelligence but require corroboration before use.

### Expat Community Publications

| Source | Why lower confidence | Acceptable use |
|---|---|---|
| Expatica Cyprus guides | Not always current; may not distinguish between permit types | Background context only; must be corroborated |
| International Living Cyprus | Promotional orientation; may oversimplify | Background only |
| Cyprus Expat Forum (official groups) | Unattributed; anecdotal | Identify questions and themes only — never cite |

### News Media

| Source | Why lower confidence | Acceptable use |
|---|---|---|
| Cyprus Mail | Journalistic accuracy good but not regulatory precision | Breaking news about regulatory changes; then verify via official source |
| Financial Mirror (Cyprus) | Financial/business focus; generally reliable | Business and tax news leads |
| In-Cyprus | Mixed quality | As lead only |

---

## Unacceptable Sources

The following must not be used as sources for any Paphos Advisors knowledge content:

| Source Type | Reason |
|---|---|
| Reddit (r/cyprus, r/digitalnomad, etc.) | Unattributed, anecdotal, jurisdiction errors common |
| Quora | No accountability; frequently outdated |
| Anonymous blog posts | No accountability; often SEO-driven content without factual rigour |
| AI-generated summaries (ChatGPT, Perplexity without citations) | Cannot verify source; may hallucinate specifics |
| Content farm articles | No editorial standard |
| Facebook comments (including expat groups) | Unattributed; high error rate |
| Competitor advisory websites | Potential conflicts of interest; unverifiable |
| Wikipedia | Not authoritative for regulatory information |

**Note on Perplexity:** Perplexity is acceptable as a research tool when it cites its sources clearly. The Perplexity citations must be independently verifiable. The Perplexity summary itself is not the source — the underlying cited documents are.

## Partner Knowledge as a Source

Partner knowledge (from interviews and interactions) is treated as a distinct source tier — see `research/frameworks/field-intelligence-framework.md` for the full framework.

Summary:
- Tier 1 partner knowledge (structured interview with vetted, active partner): confidence `low–medium` for field intelligence claims
- Informal partner comment: confidence `low`
- Partner knowledge contradicting official guidance: document both; flag for resolution; do not use contested claim in published content

---

## Source Conflict Resolution

When a Tier 1 official source and a Tier 2 or Tier 3 source conflict:

1. The official source takes precedence for regulatory rules
2. The field/practitioner source may be valid for *practical experience* — document both positions clearly
3. If the official source appears outdated (e.g., dated guidance that practitioners uniformly contradict), flag for revalidation — do not silently prefer the field source
4. When in doubt: `confidence: unverified` and schedule for partner verification

See `system/standards/source-citation-standards.md` for citation formatting rules.
