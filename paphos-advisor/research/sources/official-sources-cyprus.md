# Official Sources — Cyprus (RES-SRC-001)

## Purpose
Catalogues the primary official sources used for research across Paphos Advisors' practice areas. These are the sources that produce `confidence: high` knowledge when cited directly and verified within 12 months.

---

## Immigration and Residency

### Civil Registry and Migration Department (CRMD)
- **URL:** `crmd.moi.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — primary authority for all residence permit applications
- **What it covers:** Yellow Slip (MEU1), Pink Slip (MEU3), Digital Nomad Visa, Category F, Category 6.2, ARC cards, temporary residence permits
- **Key sections:** Application forms, document checklists, fee schedules, office locations and hours
- **Caution:** Information is not always current; always verify against recent partner intelligence
- **Languages:** Greek primary; some English sections

### Ministry of Interior — Cyprus
- **URL:** `moi.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — policy authority above CRMD
- **What it covers:** Immigration policy, circulars, press releases on residency changes
- **Key sections:** Press releases section for recent changes
- **Languages:** Greek primary

### EU Free Movement of Workers — EURES
- **URL:** `eures.europa.eu`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — EU authority for EU national freedom of movement rights
- **What it covers:** EU citizen rights to work and reside in Cyprus
- **Key sections:** Cyprus country profile

### European Commission — Migration and Home Affairs
- **URL:** `ec.europa.eu/home-affairs`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — EU law basis for immigration rules
- **What it covers:** Freedom of movement directive (2004/38/EC), third-country national rules
- **Key sections:** Country compliance reports

## Taxation

### Cyprus Tax Department (Inland Revenue)
- **URL:** `taxisnet.mof.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — primary tax authority
- **What it covers:** Personal income tax, corporate tax, Non-Dom regime, GESY contributions, tax registration, filing deadlines
- **Key sections:** Legislation, circulars, forms, tax rates page
- **Caution:** Rate pages may not reflect mid-year changes; check circulars section for amendments
- **Languages:** Greek primary; English translations available for major guidance

### Ministry of Finance — Cyprus
- **URL:** `mof.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — policy authority for all fiscal matters
- **What it covers:** Annual budget announcements, tax reform proposals, press releases
- **Key sections:** Press releases, budget documents

### ICPAC (Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Cyprus)
- **URL:** `icpac.org.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 2 — professional body for accountants; provides interpretation of tax rules
- **What it covers:** Accounting standards, tax compliance guidance, GESY, company audit requirements
- **Key sections:** Publications, circulars, guidance notes
- **Note:** Use for practitioner interpretation, not as primary source for rules themselves

## Property

### Department of Lands and Surveys
- **URL:** `dls.moi.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — primary authority for property registration, title deeds, transfer fees
- **What it covers:** Title deed registration, property transfer process, fee schedule, land registry
- **Key sections:** Fees and charges, application forms, legislation
- **Caution:** Transfer fee structures have changed multiple times; always verify current fees

### Ministry of Interior — Property Division
- **URL:** `moi.gov.cy` (property section)
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — policy authority for property legislation
- **What it covers:** Immovable property legislation, title deed amnesty schemes

### CREAA (Cyprus Real Estate Agents Association)
- **URL:** `creaa.org.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 2 — regulatory body for licensed property agents
- **What it covers:** Agent licensing, professional standards, property market guidance
- **Note:** Use to verify agent registration status, not as source for property rules

---

## Business Formation

### Registrar of Companies — Cyprus
- **URL:** `efiling.drcor.mcit.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — primary authority for company registration
- **What it covers:** HE company formation, annual returns, dissolution, director/shareholder registers
- **Key sections:** Company search, registration forms, fee schedule, legislation

### Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry
- **URL:** `mcit.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — policy authority for commercial law and company regulation
- **What it covers:** Companies Law cap 113, business licensing, trade names

### Cyprus Bar Association
- **URL:** `cybar.org.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 2 — regulatory body for legal professionals
- **What it covers:** Lawyer registration (required for immigration and property work), professional conduct
- **Note:** Use to verify lawyer registration status

### Central Bank of Cyprus
- **URL:** `centralbank.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — regulatory authority for banking and AML compliance
- **What it covers:** AML/KYC requirements, banking licensing, company banking compliance
- **Note:** Relevant for company banking difficulties — AML policy affects account opening

## Healthcare

### General Healthcare System — GESY / HIO
- **URL:** `gesy.org.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — primary authority for GESY registration and contribution rules
- **What it covers:** GESY registration eligibility, contribution rates, enrolled provider lists
- **Key sections:** Contribution rates, registration forms, beneficiary rules
- **Caution:** Non-Dom and part-year resident rules are not prominently documented; verify with Tax Department for contribution obligations

### Ministry of Health — Cyprus
- **URL:** `moh.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — policy authority for healthcare system
- **What it covers:** Health policy, GESY reform, healthcare legislation

### Cyprus Medical Council
- **URL:** `cymc.org.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 2 — regulatory body for medical professionals
- **Note:** Use to verify healthcare provider registration status

## Legal Basis

### Official Gazette of Cyprus
- **URL:** `gazette.gov.cy`
- **Authority:** Tier 1 — definitive source for all enacted legislation and regulations
- **What it covers:** All new laws, regulations, circulars, government notices
- **How to use:** Search by topic, date, or law number
- **Languages:** Greek only
- **Note:** For formal legal basis; requires translation for English-language documentation
