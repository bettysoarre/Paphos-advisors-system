## Summary

Long-Term Resident (LTR) status is a permanent residence right under EU Directive 2003/109/EC, available to non-EU nationals who have held **5 consecutive years of legal residence** in Cyprus. Unlike Category F (which prohibits work and has a 5–7 year processing backlog), LTR status grants **full employment rights** in Cyprus and processes in 6–12 months. It is the natural progression for Pink Slip holders after 5 years of annual renewals, and for employment permit holders after 5 years of legal work. A key requirement is **A2-level Greek language proficiency** — no exceptions. Once renewed after the initial 5-year card, the status becomes indefinitely valid. LTR holders also gain EU mobility rights (90-day stays in other EU member states without a visa).

---

## Who This Applies To

LTR status is open to **non-EU, non-EEA, non-Swiss nationals** who:
- Have held **5 consecutive years of legal residence** in Cyprus under a qualifying permit
- Can demonstrate stable income sufficient to support themselves without public assistance
- Pass an A2-level Greek language test from the official Cyprus Examinations Authority
- Have a clean criminal record
- Have not been absent from Cyprus for more than 6 consecutive months, or more than 10 months total, during the 5-year qualifying period

**Qualifying permit types for the 5-year count:**
- Pink Slip (Temporary Residence — Visitor Category)
- Employment work permit
- Family reunification permit
- Regulation 6(2) investment PR holders (if resident 5 years)

**Does NOT count toward the 5 years:**
- Student residence permits (generally)
- Temporary protection status
- Asylum seeker status
- Humanitarian visa categories

**This is the recommended long-term route for:**
- Pink Slip holders after 5 annual renewals who want permanent status with employment rights
- Work permit holders after 5 years who want security of tenure without employer dependency

## Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1 — Complete the Greek language qualification**

Register and sit the A2 Greek language exam through the Cyprus Examinations Authority. This is a non-negotiable requirement — no alternative certificate is accepted. Allow 2–4 months if starting from scratch; existing A2 holders can proceed immediately. The civic knowledge test is taken alongside or shortly after the language exam.

**Step 2 — Prepare documentation**

Assemble the full document pack. Gather 5 years of residence permit history, criminal records from all relevant countries (apostilled), income documentation, and health insurance. Allow 4–8 weeks for foreign document procurement.

**Step 3 — Submit application to CRMD**

Submit Form MLT3 and the full document pack in person at the local CRMD district office. Pay the €30 fee and receive a submission receipt.

**Step 4 — Biometric appointment**

Attend a biometric appointment (fingerprints, photograph) at the CRMD. This must be done in person in Cyprus — it cannot be completed remotely.

**Step 5 — Background verification**

The CRMD conducts international background checks. This stage typically takes 2–4 months. An interview may be requested at the CRMD's discretion.

**Step 6 — Decision and card collection**

On approval, collect the LTR residence card from the CRMD. The initial card is valid for 5 years. Pay the card fee at collection.

**Step 7 — Renewal (once only)**

After 5 years, submit a renewal application using the same MLT3 form. Updated documentation required (health insurance, criminal records, proof of continued residence and income). On renewal, the status becomes **indefinitely valid** — no further renewals needed.

## Maintaining the Status

LTR status is maintained by:

- **Not being absent from the EU for more than 6 consecutive years** — absence for this duration results in automatic loss of LTR status
- **Not being absent from Cyprus for more than 12 months** — shorter absences are permitted with advance authorisation for exceptional reasons
- **Renewing the card every 5 years** (until first renewal, at which point status becomes indefinite)
- **Maintaining health insurance** and accommodation
- **Criminal record updates** every 3 years

**Employment rights:** Unlike Category F and the Pink Slip, LTR holders have **full employment rights** — they can work for any employer in any occupation without restriction. This is a significant difference from the financially independent routes.

## Field Notes

- **The A2 Greek test is the critical bottleneck.** Many applicants underestimate the time needed to pass the A2 exam. Start preparation early — ideally 12+ months before the 5-year qualifying period ends. There are no exemptions, not even for long-term residents who have "effectively integrated."
- **LTR beats Category F for active people.** If an applicant wants to work, start a business, or keep employment options open in Cyprus, LTR is dramatically better than Category F. The only advantage Category F has is that employment prohibition is the applicant's situation by choice, not a legal restriction.
- **Pink Slip → LTR is the cleanest non-EU pathway.** For UK retirees and non-EU self-sufficient residents, 5 years of Pink Slip renewals followed by an LTR application gives indefinite permanent status with employment rights and EU mobility. This is better than waiting indefinitely for Category F.
- **EU mobility is a significant benefit.** Once Cyprus accedes to Schengen (targeted 2026), LTR holders gain visa-free travel across Schengen member states for up to 90 days per 180 days — a material improvement over current third-country national status. ## Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| A2 certificate from unofficial provider | Only the Cyprus Examinations Authority certificate is accepted. Greek language schools and private tutors can prepare you for the exam, but cannot issue the qualifying certificate. |
| Gaps in the 5-year residence history | Any gap where no valid permit was held can break the 5-year continuous residence requirement. If there is a gap, legal advice is needed to assess whether it is fatal to the application. |
| Absence exceeding 6 consecutive months | Obtain advance written authorisation from the CRMD for prolonged absences. Unauthorised absences of this length can reset the 5-year clock or result in loss of status. |
| Document procurement delays | Start gathering foreign criminal records and apostilles 3–4 months before the intended submission date. International document requests can take 6–8 weeks. |
