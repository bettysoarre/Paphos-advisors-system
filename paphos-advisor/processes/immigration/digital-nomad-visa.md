## Summary

The Cyprus Digital Nomad Visa is a temporary residence permit for non-EU and non-EEA nationals who work remotely for employers or clients based entirely outside Cyprus. It is issued for one year and renewable for up to three years in total. It does not permit the holder to work for Cyprus-based employers or clients. The scheme was introduced in October 2021, was suspended after its initial 500-permit quota was reached, and was reactivated and expanded in March 2025 with an additional 500 permits (1,000 total). The application must be submitted in person in Cyprus within 3 months of arrival.

---

## Who This Applies To

The Digital Nomad Visa is open to nationals of countries **outside the EU and EEA** (so not available to EU, EEA, or Swiss nationals — they use MEU1/MEU2 routes).

**Eligibility conditions — all must be met:**
1. Non-EU / non-EEA national, aged 18 or over
2. Employed or self-employed, with the ability to perform all work duties remotely using internet and telecommunications technology
3. All employers or clients are registered and based **outside Cyprus** — no economic activity with Cyprus-registered entities is permitted
4. Minimum net monthly income of **€3,500** (after tax and deductions in the country of employment)
5. Valid passport and lawful entry to Cyprus (visa-free or via Cyprus tourist visa)

**Income requirement for families:**
- Spouse or civil partner: add 20% to the base requirement (€3,500 × 1.20 = **€4,200** minimum)
- Each dependent minor child: add a further 15% (€4,200 × 1.15 = **€4,830** for applicant + spouse + one child; additional 15% per further child)

**Who is excluded:**
- EU, EEA, and Swiss nationals (they use EU freedom of movement routes)
- Nationals intending to work for any Cyprus-based employer or client
- Applicants with criminal records for serious offences
- Applicants who have previously overstayed or violated Cyprus immigration conditions

**ICP relevance:**
- **ICP-003 (Non-EU Digital Nomad):** primary route
- **ICP-001 (UK National Retiree):** not applicable — this route requires active employment income of €3,500/month; UK retirees with passive income should consider Category F or Investment PR

## What You Will Need

### Documents Required

| Document | Format | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| MVIS8 application form | Original, fully completed | Obtain from CRMD office or official portal; all fields must be completed; must match supporting documents exactly |
| Valid passport | Original (for inspection) + copy | Minimum 3 months' validity beyond the end of the requested permit period; include entry stamp page |
| 2 passport-sized photographs | Original | 35mm × 45mm; white background; taken within 6 months |
| Curriculum vitae (CV) | Copy | Professional work history; confirms remote work capability |
| Employment contract or client contracts | Original + copy | See notes below |
| Employer confirmation letter | Original on company letterhead | See notes below |
| Copy of employer's company registration | Certified copy | Confirms employer is registered and operating outside Cyprus |
| Bank statements (6 months) | Original or certified bank printout | Must show regular deposits meeting or exceeding €3,500/month net; all 6 months |
| Recent payslips or income statements | Copy (3–6 months) | Confirms current income level and net pay; for freelancers: recent invoices or payment platform statements |
| Health insurance certificate | Original | Minimum €30,000 coverage; must include inpatient, outpatient, and emergency repatriation/medical transport; must cover the full permit period |
| Medical examination certificates | Originals | Blood serology (HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B, hepatitis C) + chest X-ray (tuberculosis); for children under 15: Mantoux skin test in place of chest X-ray; must be recent (within 3 months of application) |
| Police clearance certificate | Original + apostille (if issued abroad) + certified English translation | Issued within 6 months of application; for US nationals: FBI background check; for UK nationals: ACRO certificate |
| Proof of accommodation in Cyprus | Original | Rental agreement, lease, property title deed, or hotel booking; rental agreements above €5,000 value must be Tax Department stamped and certified by Mukhtar/notary |
| Declaration of non-engagement with Cyprus economy | Signed original | Signed statement confirming the applicant will not work for Cyprus-based entities; separate statement required for each adult family member |
| Family documents (if applicable) | Originals + apostille + certified translation | Marriage certificate (if including spouse); birth certificates for each child included in application |

**Employment documentation — detail:**
- *Employed:* employment contract explicitly authorising remote work + employer confirmation letter stating that (a) remote work from Cyprus is authorised, (b) the work involves no services to Cyprus-based entities, and (c) the employment relationship is ongoing. Letter must be on company letterhead, signed by authorised company representative.
- *Freelancer/self-employed:* client contracts or service agreements with foreign clients (covering minimum 1 year); signed personal declaration confirming remote capability and no Cyprus-based clients; company registration documents if operating as a business entity.

All documents not in English or Greek require certified professional translation + apostille from the country of issue.

### Where to Apply

Applications must be submitted **in person** in Cyprus at:

**Civil Registry and Migration Department (CRMD)**
Primary submission point: Nicosia headquarters
District offices also accept applications: Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, Famagusta

**Official portal:** [https://www.mip.gov.cy](https://www.mip.gov.cy)

- Applications cannot be submitted from outside Cyprus — you must enter Cyprus first
- The application must be submitted within **3 months of your date of entry** into Cyprus
- A legal representative may submit on the applicant's behalf (with power of attorney) but the applicant must attend separately for biometric data collection
- Appointment booking via the official CRMD portal; book as soon as you arrive in Cyprus — slots can be several weeks out

## Step-by-Step Process

**1. Enter Cyprus lawfully.** If your nationality requires a Cyprus tourist visa to enter, obtain this from a Cyprus embassy or consulate before travelling. Once in Cyprus, your 3-month application window begins from your date of entry. Do not delay.

**2. Book a CRMD appointment immediately upon arrival.** Appointment slots can be several weeks out. Book through the official portal at [https://www.mip.gov.cy](https://www.mip.gov.cy) on or within the first few days of arrival. This is the step most applicants delay too long.

**3. Secure accommodation and obtain the rental agreement.** If using a rental agreement, ensure it covers the permit period. Rental agreements above €5,000 in value must be stamped by the Tax Department and certified by a Mukhtar or notary.

**4. Complete the MVIS8 application form.** Obtain from the CRMD or official portal. Complete all fields in English; every entry must match supporting documents exactly.

**5. Obtain medical examinations.** Book a blood serology test (HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B, hepatitis C) and chest X-ray at a clinic or hospital in Cyprus. Certificates must be dated within 3 months of your application submission. Do this early in the process as clinic availability varies.

**6. Gather and authenticate all supporting documents.** Assemble the complete document list above. For any documents not already in English, arrange certified professional translation. Apostille any foreign documents where required (police clearance certificates, marriage/birth certificates).

**7. Prepare the employer confirmation letter and declaration.** Request the confirmation letter from your employer. Draft and sign the declaration of non-engagement with the Cyprus economy.

**8. Attend the CRMD appointment in person.** Bring all originals and copies, the completed MVIS8 form, 2 passport photographs, and the applicable government fees (€140 per person). Biometric data (fingerprints and photograph) will be collected at this appointment.

**9. Receive your receipt and await the decision.** Processing typically takes 5–7 weeks from submission of a complete application. The CRMD will notify you when the residence permit card is ready for collection. Remain in Cyprus during the processing period.

**10. Collect your Digital Nomad Visa residence permit card.** Attend the CRMD office in person to collect the card. The card confirms your authorised residence in Cyprus for the stated period.

### Timeline

**Application must be submitted:** Within 3 months of entry into Cyprus.

**Official processing time:** 5–7 weeks from submission of a complete application.

**Real-world processing time (2026):** 4–12 weeks, depending on completeness of documentation and nationality (background check times vary).

**Permit validity:** 1 year from date of issue.

**Renewal:** Up to 2 additional renewals (each for 1 year), giving a maximum total of 3 years under this category. Renewal applications should be submitted at least 1 month before expiry.

⚠ Quota limitation: The current scheme permits 1,000 total residence permits. As of March 2025 (scheme reactivation), an additional 500 permits were available. ## Field Notes

> **These notes reflect field intelligence from practitioner sources and reported real-world experience. They are not official guidance and may vary by officer, office, and timing. Each note should be independently verified.**

**FN-001 — Quota risk: scheme has been suspended before (2026)**
The Digital Nomad Visa scheme was suspended after its original 500 permits were taken up. It was reactivated in March 2025 with an additional 500 permits. If this quota is reached, the scheme will suspend again. Applicants who are ready to apply should not delay.
*Source: Hartsiotis Law firm announcement, March 2025. [NEEDS VALIDATION: confirm current remaining quota]*

**FN-002 — Family members CANNOT work — even remotely (2026)**
This restriction surprises many applicants. A spouse or partner accompanying the primary applicant under this scheme cannot maintain any employment or self-employment — including remote work for foreign employers. If both spouses are remote workers, each must apply for their own Digital Nomad Visa in their own right (each meeting the €3,500 minimum income requirement separately). Violation of this condition is grounds for permit revocation.
*Source: Koufettas Law, KPMG, multiple practitioner sources, 2026.*

**FN-003 — Employment contract must explicitly authorise remote work (2026)**
Employment contracts that do not mention remote work are a common rejection trigger. Many contracts predating the remote-work era simply state office location. Applicants should obtain a supplementary letter or contract amendment explicitly confirming remote work authorisation before applying.
*Source: Multiple practitioner sources, 2026.*

**FN-004 — Tax residency implications (183-day rule)**
Any person physically present in Cyprus for more than 183 days in a calendar year becomes a Cyprus tax resident for that year. Digital Nomad Visa holders who intend to be present year-round should take advice on their Cyprus tax position, particularly regarding the non-dom exemption (17-year exemption from Special Defence Contribution on passive income such as dividends and interest). See PROC-TAX-002.
*Source: Standard Cyprus tax practitioner guidance, 2026.*

**FN-005 — 90-day absence limit**
Holders who leave Cyprus for more than 90 consecutive days risk permit cancellation, as authorities may conclude Cyprus is not their primary residence. Short holidays and trips are fine; extended periods abroad should be discussed with a lawyer before departure.
*Source: Multiple practitioner sources, 2026. [NEEDS VALIDATION: confirm whether this is strictly enforced in practice or primarily a precautionary note]*

**FN-006 — Appointment booking is the critical first action**
Practitioners consistently report that applicants waste weeks of their 3-month application window before booking an appointment. With slots sometimes 4–6 weeks out and documents needing authentication, time runs out quickly. Book the appointment within the first week of arrival.
*Source: Practitioner guidance and community reports, 2026.*

## Common Issues and How to Handle Them

**Issue:** Employment contract does not mention remote work.
**Resolution:** Request a supplementary employer letter or contract addendum explicitly authorising remote work from Cyprus before submitting the application.

**Issue:** Application submitted after the 3-month window.
**Resolution:** Exit Cyprus and re-enter to restart the 3-month window. Take advice before re-entering to confirm this approach is appropriate for your circumstances.

**Issue:** Bank statements show income below €3,500/month in some months.
**Resolution:** Immigration authorities look at the pattern. A month or two below the threshold is less likely to cause problems than a consistent pattern of under-threshold deposits. If income is irregular (freelance), supplement with contracts, invoices, and a certified accountant statement confirming annual income and average monthly earnings.

**Issue:** Spouse's application rejected because they have remote employment income.
**Resolution:** The spouse must apply separately as a primary applicant under the Digital Nomad Visa, meeting all eligibility criteria independently (including the income minimum). Being included as a "dependent" while maintaining employment income is not permitted.

**Issue:** Permit not renewed before expiry.
**Resolution:** Begin the renewal process at least 1 month before expiry. If the permit has lapsed, the applicant may need to leave Cyprus and re-enter to apply fresh (or seek legal advice on whether a late renewal is possible). Do not allow the permit to expire without action.

## Permitted and Prohibited Activities

| Permitted | Prohibited |
|-----------|------------|
| Remote work for foreign employers | Any work for Cyprus-registered employers |
| Freelance work for foreign clients | Freelance work for Cyprus-based clients |
| Receiving income from foreign sources | Providing services (paid or unpaid) to Cyprus entities |
| Investing in property in Cyprus | Salaried employment in Cyprus |
| Holding shares in foreign companies | Operating a Cyprus-registered business |
| Serving as director of a foreign company | Volunteering professional services to Cyprus organisations |
| Opening a Cyprus bank account | — |
| Travelling within the EU using the residence permit card | — |

## Related Processes

- **PROC-TAX-001 — 60-Day Tax Residency Rule:** Relevant if the holder is also seeking to establish Cyprus tax residency to benefit from the non-dom regime. The Digital Nomad Visa does not automatically confer tax residency — this depends on physical presence.
- **PROC-TAX-002 — Non-Dom Status:** Digital Nomad Visa holders who become Cyprus tax residents may qualify for 17-year non-dom exemption on dividends, interest, and rental income. Separate process.
- **PROC-HLTH-001 — GESY Registration:** GESY (public health system) registration is typically not available to Digital Nomad Visa holders who are not employed in Cyprus and contributing to the social insurance system. Private health insurance is required throughout.
