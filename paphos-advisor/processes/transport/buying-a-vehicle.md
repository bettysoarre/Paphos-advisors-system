## Summary

Buying a vehicle in Cyprus — whether from a dealer or private seller — requires a transfer of ownership registered with the Road Transport Department (RTD), valid Cyprus insurance, and annual road tax. The process is straightforward for dealer purchases (dealer handles registration paperwork) and slightly more manual for private sales (buyer and seller attend RTD together or use an authorised agent). Cyprus has a large second-hand car market with many right-hand-drive vehicles, making it particularly accessible for UK nationals. New arrivals who do not bring a vehicle from their home country typically buy locally within the first month.

---

## Who This Applies To

Residents of Cyprus purchasing a vehicle locally. Includes both new arrivals (first vehicle purchase) and existing residents purchasing additional or replacement vehicles.

---

## Purchase Options

### New Vehicle (Dealer)
- Full new vehicle warranty
- Dealer registers the vehicle in the buyer's name — buyer provides ID, residency document, and Cyprus address
- VAT (19%) included in price
- Financing available through dealer or Cypriot banks
- Road tax paid at time of registration; insurance must be in place before driving off the lot

### Used Vehicle (Dealer)
- Same registration process as new — dealer handles transfer
- Pre-sale roadworthy inspection typically done by dealer; ask for confirmation
- No VAT on second-hand private imports resold by dealers — price is inclusive

### Private Sale
- Buyer and seller complete transfer jointly at RTD (or via an authorised agent)
- Buyer must arrange Cyprus insurance before transfer can be completed
- Check for outstanding road tax, fines, or encumbrances before purchase

## Private Sale — Transfer of Ownership Process

1. **Agree the sale** — agree price; confirm the vehicle has no outstanding road tax debt, fines, or liens [NEEDS VALIDATION — confirm how to run an encumbrance check; RTD may provide a vehicle history check]
2. **Obtain Cyprus insurance** — contact a Cyprus insurer with the vehicle's registration details; obtain at minimum third-party liability cover
3. **Attend RTD together** — buyer and seller (or their authorised representative) attend the local RTD office; Paphos RTD for Paphos district
4. **Complete transfer forms** — RTD transfer form to be completed and signed by both parties
5. **Pay transfer fee** — a small administrative fee applies [NEEDS VALIDATION — confirm current transfer fee]
6. **Pay road tax** — annual road tax due at point of transfer if not already paid for the current year
7. **Receive updated registration certificate** — vehicle is registered in buyer's name

**Documents required (buyer):**
- Passport or national ID
- Residency permit / Yellow Slip / Category F card
- Cyprus address proof
- Insurance certificate

**Documents required (seller):**
- Passport or national ID
- Current vehicle registration certificate

## Road Tax

Annual road tax (τέλη κυκλοφορίας) must be paid before the vehicle can legally be driven. It is based on CO2 emissions for post-2013 vehicles or engine displacement for older vehicles. See PROC-TRN-001 for the rate table.

Road tax can be renewed online (JCC Smart or RTD portal), at RTD offices, at post offices, or at authorised bank branches.

**Important:** Driving without valid road tax is a fineable offence in Cyprus. Road tax expiry is checked at police stops and is visible in the RTD system.

## MOT / Roadworthy Test

Cyprus requires periodic roadworthy testing (Τεχνικός Έλεγχος) for all vehicles:

| Vehicle Age | Test Frequency |
|------------|---------------|
| Under 4 years | Exempt |
| 4–10 years | Every 2 years |

## Insurance

Cyprus insurance requirements:
- Third-party liability (TPL) is the legal minimum
- Comprehensive cover strongly recommended for new arrivals unfamiliar with local roads
- Premiums typically lower than UK equivalents for equivalent cover
- Insurers require: driver's licence (or equivalent), residency status, vehicle registration details
- No-claims discount from UK or other EU policies may be recognised by some Cyprus insurers — confirm with each insurer individually

## Notes for Agents

- New arrivals frequently need to buy a vehicle quickly. Flag that insurance must be in place before the RTD transfer can complete — arranging insurance the day before the RTD appointment avoids delays.
- The second-hand market in Cyprus skews heavily right-hand drive, which benefits UK clients. EU nationals arriving from left-hand-drive countries may need time to adjust.
- Advise clients to check road tax and outstanding fine status before paying a deposit on a private sale — encumbrances transfer with the vehicle if not cleared.
- No-claims history from the UK is worth asking about with each insurer — not universally recognised but sometimes accepted, which reduces first-year premiums.
- For clients who are not yet Cyprus residents (still in the application process), a vehicle can be purchased but may need to be registered in a different name temporarily — flag this edge case and refer to a lawyer if relevant.
