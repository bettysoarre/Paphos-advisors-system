## Summary

Bringing a vehicle to Cyprus requires navigating customs clearance, a roadworthy inspection, and re-registration with the Road Transport Department (RTD). The rules differ significantly depending on origin: EU vehicles imported by EU nationals benefit from simplified rules; UK vehicles face post-Brexit customs considerations; all non-EU vehicles face standard import duty and VAT. Cyprus drives on the left — a practical advantage for UK drivers that eliminates the need for left-hand drive conversion. Re-registration must be completed within the timeframe permitted by temporary import rules; most relocating residents need to finalise import within 6–12 months of establishing residency.

---

## Who This Applies To

Residents or soon-to-be residents of Cyprus who are bringing a personal vehicle from another country. Does not apply to visitors or short-term stays (tourists can drive on foreign plates for up to 3 months).

## Step-by-Step Process

### Phase 1 — Customs Clearance

1. **Arrive with the vehicle** — drive or ship to Cyprus (Limassol port for shipped vehicles)
2. **Submit customs declaration** at Cyprus Customs with:
   - Vehicle registration certificate (V5C for UK; registration document for other countries)
   - Proof of purchase / ownership history
   - Proof of VAT payment in country of origin (for EU vehicles claiming VAT relief)
   - Evidence of prior use (6+ months for personal effects relief)
   - Proof of Cyprus residency (residency permit or Yellow Slip)
3. **Pay any applicable duty and VAT** — or obtain relief approval if eligible
4. **Receive customs clearance certificate**

### Phase 2 — Roadworthy Inspection

5. **Book an RTD roadworthy test** (Έλεγχος Οχήματος) — appointment-based at RTD stations in Paphos
6. **Pass the roadworthy test** — vehicle must meet Cyprus/EU safety standards; common failure points include tyre condition, lighting, emissions
7. **Address any failures** — minor issues can often be corrected same day; major failures require repair before retest

### Phase 3 — RTD Re-Registration

8. **Submit re-registration application** to RTD with:
   - Original foreign registration certificate
   - Customs clearance certificate
   - Roadworthy test pass certificate
   - Valid insurance certificate (Cyprus-registered insurer required)
   - Proof of identity and Cyprus address
   - Payment of first year's road tax
9. **Receive Cyprus registration plates and certificate**
10. **Return foreign plates** to the issuing country authority if required (UK: DVLA)

## Insurance Before Registration

**Cyprus insurance is required before RTD will register the vehicle.** You cannot obtain Cyprus registration without it.

- Contact a Cyprus-based insurer or broker before your RTD appointment
- Minimum third-party liability insurance required; comprehensive recommended
- UK insurance does not remain valid for Cyprus residents after residency is established
- Insurers will require the foreign registration details to issue a pre-registration quote

## Notes for Agents

- The most common mistake is arriving in Cyprus with a vehicle and doing nothing for months, assuming temporary import status continues indefinitely. It does not — residency triggers the re-registration obligation.
- UK clients benefit from driving on the left — no steering wheel conversion needed. Headlight beam deflectors may be needed for UK cars driving in EU in transit; not required once re-registered in Cyprus.
- For clients with high-emission vehicles (diesel SUVs, older petrol engines), run a road tax estimate before they commit to importing — it can be a significant annual cost.
- The personal effects relief is valuable but must be claimed at the time of customs clearance, not retrospectively. Flag this before arrival.
- For high-value or classic vehicles, recommend specialist shipping and professional customs clearance rather than self-clearing.
