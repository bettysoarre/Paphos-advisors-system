# Transport Processes

Processes covering vehicle import, purchase, registration, road tax, and driving in Cyprus.

---

## Processes in This Domain

| ID | Title | Status | Notes |
|----|-------|--------|-------|
| PROC-TRN-001 | Vehicle Import | draft | Bringing a car from UK, EU, or non-EU; customs, roadworthy, RTD re-registration |
| PROC-TRN-002 | Buying a Vehicle in Cyprus | draft | Private sale and dealer purchase; transfer of ownership, road tax, insurance |

**Cross-reference:** Driving licence exchange is documented in the Settling-In domain as **PROC-SET-002** — it is relevant to both transport and settling-in.

---

## Recommended Order

1. **PROC-SET-002 — Driving Licence Exchange** — Book the appointment immediately on arrival; 2–3 month wait in Paphos. Do not delay.
2. **PROC-TRN-001 — Vehicle Import** (if bringing a car) — Initiate customs clearance and roadworthy inspection within the first month; temporary import rules have time limits once residency is established.
3. **PROC-TRN-002 — Buying a Vehicle** (if buying locally) — Arrange insurance first, then RTD transfer. Most new arrivals need a vehicle within the first 2–4 weeks.

---

## Key Points for Agents

- **Cyprus drives on the left.** UK clients have no adjustment required. EU nationals from left-hand-drive countries need time to adapt.
- **Right-hand-drive second-hand market.** The Cypriot used car market skews heavily RHD, which helps UK clients find familiar vehicles at reasonable prices.
- **Re-registration is mandatory.** Residents cannot indefinitely drive on foreign plates. Once residency is established, re-registration must be completed within the permitted window — advise clients not to ignore this.
- **Insurance before registration.** Whether importing or buying locally, Cyprus insurance must be in place before RTD will complete any registration.
