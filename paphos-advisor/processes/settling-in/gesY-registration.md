## Summary

GeSY (Γενικό Σύστημα Υγείας — General Healthcare System) is Cyprus's universal public healthcare system, launched in 2019. All Cyprus tax residents are both **eligible** and **obligated** to contribute to GeSY. Registration gives access to subsidised GP visits, specialist referrals, emergency care, and hospital treatment through the public network. Registration is mandatory for employed and self-employed residents; it is handled automatically for employees through their employer's payroll but must be done actively by self-employed individuals and other tax residents. The contribution is income-based and capped — for non-dom dividend-extracting company directors, the maximum annual GeSY liability is €4,770.

---

## Who This Applies To

- All **Cyprus tax residents** — employed, self-employed, company directors, retirees drawing Cyprus-source income
- **Non-EU Pink Slip holders** and other visa categories who are Cyprus tax resident
- **EU nationals** with Yellow Slip and Cyprus tax residency

**Note on Pink Slip holders:** Pink Slip holders who are Cyprus tax resident must register and contribute to GeSY. However, the Pink Slip health insurance requirement (Plan A minimum €13,669/year coverage) is a **separate** private insurance requirement — GeSY registration does not replace it. Pink Slip holders typically hold both GeSY registration and private health insurance.

## Contribution Rates (2026)

All rates are subject to annual revision. Confirm current rates with your accountant.

| Income Type | Employee / Individual Rate | Employer Rate (if employed) | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employment income | 2.65% | 2.90% | No cap on employment income |
| Self-employment income | 4.00% | — | [NEEDS VALIDATION — confirm self-employed GeSY rate] |
| Dividend income | 2.65% | — | Capped at €180,000 income (max €4,770/year) |
| Pension income | 2.65% | — | [NEEDS VALIDATION] |
| Rental income | 2.65% | — | [NEEDS VALIDATION] |

**For non-dom company directors extracting dividends:** The 2.65% GeSY contribution on dividend income is the only healthcare levy that applies under non-dom status. Maximum annual liability is €4,770 regardless of dividend amount above €180,000. This is not a tax — it buys access to the GeSY network.

## The Process

### Employed Individuals
GeSY contributions are deducted automatically from salary through payroll. Your employer registers you and handles contributions. You still need to **register as a GeSY beneficiary** to access services — do this through the [GeSY portal](https://www.gesy.org.cy) or at a GeSY district office.

### Self-Employed and Company Directors

1. **Obtain TIC first** (PROC-SET-004)
2. **Register for social insurance** (PROC-SET-005) — social insurance and GeSY registration are linked for self-employed individuals
3. **Declare income to the Tax Department** — GeSY contributions are calculated on your declared income and collected alongside income tax
4. **Register as a beneficiary** on the GeSY portal to access the network — separate from contribution registration

### Retired Residents Drawing Pension Income

1. **Obtain TIC**
2. **Register at the Tax Department** — declare pension income; GeSY contributions are collected via income tax filing
3. **Register as a GeSY beneficiary** online or at a district office

---

## Accessing GeSY Services

Once registered as a beneficiary, you can:
- **Register with a GeSY Personal Doctor (GP)** — every beneficiary must register with a specific GP; this doctor manages referrals to specialists
- **Book GP appointments** via the GeSY portal or phone
- **Access specialist care** through GP referral — most specialist consultations are partially or fully covered
- **Emergency care** — GeSY covers emergency treatment at public hospitals regardless of GP registration status

---

## GeSY vs Private Health Insurance

GeSY does not replace private health insurance for Pink Slip holders — both are required for that category. For others, GeSY provides reasonable day-to-day coverage but has waiting times for non-urgent specialist appointments. Many residents supplement GeSY with a private plan for faster specialist access and private hospital options.

---

## Notes for Agents

- The most common error is assuming GeSY registration is the same as meeting the Pink Slip private health insurance requirement. It is not. Pink Slip holders must have Plan A private insurance (min €13,669/year coverage) regardless of GeSY registration.
- Company directors who extract only dividends (no salary) must still register for GeSY and pay the 2.65% levy on dividend income via their tax return. Many new clients are unaware of this until their first tax filing.
- Self-employed GeSY contribution rates differ from employed rates — always confirm with Demetris for the current year's figures before quoting clients.
