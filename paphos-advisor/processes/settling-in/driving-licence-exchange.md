## Summary

Cyprus requires all residents to exchange their foreign driving licence for a Cyprus licence once they establish residency. The exchange process is handled by the Department of Road Transport. Appointment lead times are typically **2–3 months**, so residents should book as soon as they arrive — do not wait until the foreign licence is close to expiry. You can legally drive on a valid foreign licence while the exchange is in progress. The difficulty of the exchange depends on nationality: EU/EEA licences exchange straightforwardly; UK licences require additional verification with the DVLA; non-EU licences from countries without a bilateral exchange agreement require a theory test.

---

## Who This Applies To

Anyone who:
- Has established residency in Cyprus (has a Yellow Slip, Pink Slip, ARC card, or equivalent)
- Holds a valid foreign driving licence and wishes to drive in Cyprus long-term

**Note:** EU/EEA nationals visiting Cyprus (not resident) can drive on their EU licence indefinitely. The exchange obligation arises when you become a **resident**.

**For UK licence holders:** The Department of Road Transport will write to the DVLA to verify your licence. You cannot expedite this — it typically takes 4–8 additional weeks beyond the standard processing time. You can continue to drive on your UK licence during this period.

## The Process

1. **Book an appointment** at the Paphos Department of Road Transport (or nearest district office) — appointments book 2–3 months ahead; book immediately on arrival
2. **Obtain a medical certificate** from any licensed Cyprus GP or private doctor — this is a standard vision and fitness check, not an extensive examination; most clinics offer same-day appointments
3. **Attend your appointment** with the full document pack
4. **Original licence is retained** — the Department keeps your foreign licence as part of the exchange; you will receive a temporary paper confirmation
5. **Cyprus licence issued** — typically 2–4 weeks after the appointment; you will be notified by post or phone; collect from the Department of Road Transport

## Timeline

| Stage | Duration |
|---|---|
| Appointment lead time | 2–3 months |
| Processing (EU/EEA) | 2–4 weeks from appointment |
| Processing (UK) | 6–12 weeks from appointment (DVLA verification adds time) |
| Processing (non-EU, theory test required) | Variable — must pass theory test before exchange proceeds |

**Total time from arrival to Cyprus licence in hand: 3–5 months is typical for EU/EEA and UK nationals.**

---

## Driving on Your Foreign Licence in the Interim

You can legally drive in Cyprus on a valid foreign licence while the exchange is pending. Cyprus accepts EU, UK, and most national licences for driving during the exchange process. Keep your residency document with you when driving — if stopped, you may be asked why you have not yet exchanged your licence, and having proof that the process is underway avoids complications.

---

## Notes for Agents

- The 2–3 month appointment lead time catches many new residents by surprise. Advise clients to book on or within the first week of arrival.
- UK clients frequently ask whether Brexit has made the exchange impossible. It has not — it has only added a DVLA verification step that extends the timeline.
- Non-EU clients from countries without a bilateral exchange agreement should expect to take a theory test in English. The test is computer-based with multiple-choice questions on Cyprus traffic rules. Preparation materials are available online. [NEEDS VALIDATION — confirm current test format and pass mark]
- Clients who have already surrendered their foreign licence to the Department should be advised that they cannot drive until the Cyprus licence is issued (no foreign licence to present).
