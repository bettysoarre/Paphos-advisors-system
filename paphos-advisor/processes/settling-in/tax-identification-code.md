## Summary

A Cyprus Tax Identification Code (TIC) is a unique 8-digit number issued by the Tax Department to every individual and company with a tax obligation or financial presence in Cyprus. It is a prerequisite for almost every other settling-in process: opening a bank account, registering for GeSY, filing tax returns, registering for VAT, and completing many property and company transactions. Obtaining a personal TIC in person at a Tax Department office is typically a **same-day process** — bring the right documents and you leave with your TIC number. This should be one of the first things done after arriving in Cyprus.

---

## Who This Applies To

- All individuals establishing Cyprus tax residency
- All individuals buying property, opening bank accounts, or engaging in any financial activity in Cyprus
- All Cyprus companies (handled as part of company formation by the lawyer/accountant — see PROC-BIZ-001)
- Non-EU nationals on arrival permits (ARC card, work permit, student visa) who need a TIC for employer or bank purposes

---

## What You Will Need

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| Valid passport | Original — they may take a copy |
| Residency document | Yellow Slip, Pink Slip, ARC card, Digital Nomad card, or equivalent — or proof of pending application |
| Cyprus address proof | Rental agreement or utility bill with your name |
| Completed application form (TD.2001) | Available at the Tax Department counter or downloadable from the Tax Department website |

**Without a residency document:** If your residency application is pending, take a copy of your application receipt or appointment confirmation. The Tax Department typically issues a TIC on this basis, as the TIC is needed to complete other settling-in steps before the permit is issued.

---

## The Process

1. **Visit your district Tax Department office** — the Paphos Tax Department is on [address NEEDS VALIDATION]. No appointment is required for a TIC application — walk in.
2. **Collect and complete form TD.2001** at the counter (or download and pre-fill from the Tax Department website)
3. **Submit with supporting documents** — passport, address proof, and residency document (or pending application evidence)
4. **TIC issued same day** in most cases — you receive a printed confirmation; your accountant will use this number for all subsequent filings
5. **Keep your TIC document safe** — you will need the number for bank accounts, GeSY, and all tax filings

| Step | Duration |
|---|---|
| In-person visit | 30–60 minutes including queue time |

---

## Using Your TIC

Once issued, your TIC is used for:

| Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|
| Bank account opening | Banks require TIC for resident accounts |
| GeSY registration | Tax Department collects GeSY contributions against your TIC |
| Annual tax return filing | All Cyprus income tax returns are filed under your TIC |
| Non-dom election (TD.38) | Filed under TIC alongside annual return |
| VAT registration | Applicable if turnover exceeds VAT threshold |
| Property transactions | Required on all sale/purchase contracts registered with the Land Registry |
| Employer filings | Employers report employee income to the Tax Department using employee TICs |

---

## Company TIC

A Cyprus company's TIC is issued as part of the company registration process — the lawyer or accountant handles this. It is separate from the director's personal TIC. Both are needed for company operations.

---

## Notes for Agents

- Getting the TIC on day one or two of arrival is the highest-leverage early action for any new resident. It unblocks bank account opening, GeSY, and social insurance registration simultaneously.
- Some clients arrive and attempt to open a bank account before obtaining a TIC — they are refused. Advise them to go to the Tax Department first.
- Morning visits to the Paphos Tax Department are noticeably faster. Officers are typically more available before noon. See KA-FLD-003.
- Paphos Tax Department is considered more efficient than Nicosia for routine requests. See KA-FLD-018.
