## Recommended Order

Healthcare processes are not strictly sequential but have dependencies:

1. **PROC-HLTH-001 — Private Health Insurance** — Must be in place before submitting a Pink Slip or Category F application. Purchase or transfer to a Cyprus-compliant policy before the CRMD appointment.
2. **PROC-SET-003 → PROC-HLTH-002 — GeSY Registration then Access** — GeSY beneficiary registration (PROC-SET-003) must be completed before a Personal Doctor can be registered. Do both in the first month.
3. **PROC-HLTH-003 — Emergency Care** — Not a process to "complete" — preparation checklist only. Know the numbers and hospital locations before they are needed.

---

## Key Distinctions for Agents

- **GeSY ≠ Plan A private insurance.** Pink Slip holders must have both. GeSY satisfies day-to-day healthcare access; Plan A private insurance is the CRMD compliance requirement. These are separate.
- **The referral system.** Clients from the UK sometimes expect the occasional NHS ability to self-refer to specialists. GeSY requires a GP referral for all specialist access — no exceptions through the public system.
- **112 not 999.** UK and non-EU clients default to their home country emergency number. Flag 112 on or before day one.
