## Summary

GeSY (General Healthcare System) gives registered beneficiaries access to subsidised GP consultations, specialist referrals, diagnostic tests, prescriptions, and hospital care through the public network. Access requires two steps: first, registration as a GeSY contributor and beneficiary (see PROC-SET-003); second, registration with a **Personal Doctor** (GeSY-contracted GP). Without a registered Personal Doctor, beneficiaries can only access emergency care and walk-in GeSY services. The referral system — where the GP manages access to specialists — is central to how GeSY works. Understanding this structure prevents the most common frustration: expecting direct specialist access without a GP referral.

---

## Who This Applies To

All registered GeSY beneficiaries who want to use the public healthcare network. Registration (PROC-SET-003) must be completed first.

---

## Step 1 — Register with a Personal Doctor

Your Personal Doctor (GeSY GP) is the gateway to the full GeSY network. You must register with one before accessing routine care.

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| How to find a Personal Doctor | Via the GeSY portal (gesy.org.cy) — search by location, language, availability |
| Languages available | Greek is the primary language; some GPs offer English-language consultations — filter by language on the portal |
| Registration process | Online via GeSY portal or in person at the GP practice |
| Can I change Personal Doctor? | Yes — changes allowed periodically (check current GeSY rules for frequency) |
| Cost of GP consultation | €6 per visit (co-payment) for registered patients [NEEDS VALIDATION — confirm current co-payment amount] |

**Paphos area:** There are GeSY-contracted GPs in Paphos town and across the district. English-speaking GPs are available but may have longer registration queues.

---

## Step 2 — GP Consultation and Referrals

Once registered, your Personal Doctor handles:

| Service | Process |
|---|---|
| Routine consultations | Book via GeSY portal, phone, or in person at the practice |
| Prescription medications | GP issues electronic prescription; collect at any GeSY-registered pharmacy |
| Specialist referrals | GP issues a referral; patient books with specialist via GeSY portal |
| Diagnostic tests | GP requests tests; patient attends GeSY-registered lab |
| Chronic condition management | Ongoing care coordinated through Personal Doctor |

**The referral system:** You cannot self-refer to a GeSY specialist. Your GP must issue the referral. This is the single most important thing to understand about GeSY — clients who expect the UK's occasional ability to self-refer to NHS specialists, or the private sector's direct booking model, will find this restrictive.

## Specialist Access via GeSY

| Specialist type | Process | Typical wait time |
|---|---|---|
| Common specialties (dermatology, cardiology, orthopaedics) | GP referral → book via portal | 2–8 weeks [NEEDS VALIDATION] |
| Mental health | GP referral → GeSY-registered psychiatrist or psychologist | Longer waits — consider private for urgent needs |
| Dental | GeSY covers some dental services — check current coverage | [NEEDS VALIDATION — confirm scope of GeSY dental coverage] |
| Ophthalmology | GP referral available | [NEEDS VALIDATION] |

## Prescriptions

GeSY prescriptions are electronic. Process:

1. GP issues prescription via GeSY system during or after consultation
2. Patient collects from any GeSY-registered pharmacy (most pharmacies in Cyprus are registered)
3. Co-payment applies per prescription item [NEEDS VALIDATION — confirm current prescription co-payment]

**Chronic medications:** Patients on long-term medication can often receive repeat prescriptions without a consultation for each refill — confirm process with Personal Doctor.

---

## Hospital Care

| Type | Pathway |
|---|---|
| Planned hospital admission | GP referral or specialist recommendation → booking through GeSY |
| Emergency admission | Direct to A&E — no GP referral required (see PROC-HLTH-003) |
| Day surgery / outpatient | GP or specialist referral |

GeSY-registered public hospitals include Paphos General Hospital. Private hospitals are not part of the GeSY network (though some private clinics hold GeSY contracts for specific services).

## GeSY vs Private Healthcare — Practical Comparison

| | GeSY | Private |
|---|---|---|
| GP consultation cost | ~€6 co-payment | €40–€80 |
| Specialist consultation | Co-payment only | €80–€200+ |
| Appointment availability | GP: usually within days; specialist: 2–8 weeks | Often same week or next week |
| Language | Greek primary; English GPs available | English widely available in private sector |
| Continuity of care | Strong — Personal Doctor model | Can see different consultants each time |
| Emergency care | Covered | Covered (at higher cost unless insured) |

Many residents use GeSY for routine and GP-level care and private insurance/payment for specialist access where waiting times are unacceptable.

---

## Notes for Agents

- Clients moving from the UK sometimes expect GeSY to work exactly like the NHS — it is similar in structure (GP referral model, co-payments, public hospitals) but has shorter waiting times for most services.
- Registering with a Personal Doctor should be done as soon as possible after GeSY beneficiary registration — do not wait until health is needed.
- Paphos General Hospital is the main public hospital for Paphos district. Private options include Iasis Hospital Paphos (Agia Anastasia Hospital) — advise clients to identify both before they need them.
- For Pink Slip holders: GeSY covers day-to-day healthcare but does not replace the Plan A private insurance required for their permit. They will typically use both systems.
