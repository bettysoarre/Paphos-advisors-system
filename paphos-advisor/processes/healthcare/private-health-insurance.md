## Summary

Private health insurance is a mandatory requirement for Pink Slip and Category F applicants — CRMD will not approve an application without it. The minimum standard is **Plan A**, providing at least €13,669/year of coverage. GeSY alone does not satisfy this requirement. Beyond compliance, private insurance gives faster access to specialist consultants and private hospital care, which many residents prefer over the public system. Insurance should be purchased or transferred to a Cyprus-compliant policy before submitting a residency application, not after.

## Plan A — Minimum Standard

Plan A is the minimum coverage level accepted by CRMD for Pink Slip and Category F applications.

| Element | Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual coverage | Minimum €13,669/year | This is the coverage limit, not the premium |
| Policy type | Private health insurance | Group policies acceptable if individual coverage is confirmed |
| Geographic scope | Must cover Cyprus | Worldwide or Europe + Cyprus policies acceptable |
| Pre-existing conditions | Policy must not exclude all pre-existing conditions outright | Some exclusions acceptable; blanket exclusion policies are at risk of rejection |
| Repatriation coverage | Required for work permit and LTR applications | Check if included or if add-on required |
| Policy holder | Named individual applicant | Family members each need their own coverage confirmation |

**What CRMD checks:** The CRMD officer will inspect the policy schedule or certificate of insurance. The document must show coverage level (€13,669+ per year), the policyholder's name, and the validity period covering the application date.

## The Process

1. **Obtain quotes** — contact 2–3 insurers with the applicant's details (age, health history, required coverage level, family members)
2. **Confirm Plan A language** — the policy schedule must state coverage of at least €13,669/year; some policies state this differently (e.g., €1,140/month) — confirm with insurer how they document this for CRMD
3. **Purchase policy** — ensure the start date is before the residency application submission date
4. **Obtain policy schedule / certificate of insurance** — this is the document submitted to CRMD; it must show the policyholder name, coverage amount, and validity dates
5. **Include in application pack** — for Pink Slip: submit alongside all other documents at CRMD appointment; for Category F: same

## GeSY and Private Insurance — Not the Same

| | GeSY | Plan A Private Insurance |
|---|---|---|
| Required for Pink Slip | No | Yes — mandatory |
| Access to public GPs/hospitals | Yes | Typically no (unless policy includes hybrid access) |
| Access to private specialists | No | Yes (subject to policy terms) |
| Contribution method | Deducted via tax return | Annual or monthly premium |
| Repatriation coverage | No | Often included or available as add-on |

Pink Slip holders must have both. LTR holders can use GeSY + repatriation as an alternative to Plan A.

## Notes for Agents

- Clients sometimes arrive with a travel insurance policy and assume it counts as Plan A — it does not. Travel insurance is not health insurance and CRMD will not accept it.
- Family members applying alongside the principal must each have individual coverage documented. A family policy must confirm each member's coverage level separately.
- Premium costs should be factored into income adequacy calculations for Pink Slip — the €24,000/year income threshold should comfortably cover insurance premiums and living costs.
- Clients with serious pre-existing conditions should work with a specialist broker rather than buying direct — standard products may exclude their conditions and CRMD may reject a policy with overly broad exclusions.
