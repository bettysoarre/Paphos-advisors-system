Prompt library for AI agents used across research, content, and operations.

## Subfolders

- [agents/](agents/_index.md) — Standing instructions for deployed AI agents (PRMT-AGT-*)
- [skills/](skills/_index.md) — Modular behavioral skill files assigned to agents (SKL-*)
- [research/](research/_index.md) — Research prompts for one-off sessions
- [content/](content/_index.md) — Content drafting and editing prompts
- [analysis/](analysis/_index.md) — Analysis and gap-finding prompts
- [extraction/](extraction/_index.md) — Knowledge extraction prompts
