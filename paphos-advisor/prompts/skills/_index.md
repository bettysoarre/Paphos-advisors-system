Modular skill files for Paphos Advisors AI agents. Each skill is a reusable behavioral module that agents load alongside their core instructions. Skills are assigned to agents in `agents/_agent-skills-matrix.md`.

## Built Skills

- [skl-res.md](skl-res.md) — SKL-RES — Research with Citations (source hierarchy, confidence labelling, output format)
- [skl-seo.md](skl-seo.md) — SKL-SEO — Formatting & SEO (meta rules, heading hierarchy, schema, internal links, word counts)
- [skl-grd.md](skl-grd.md) — SKL-GRD — Brand Guardrails (immovable truths, disclaimer, British English, regulated-advice rules)
- [skl-brf.md](skl-brf.md) — SKL-BRF — Brief Writing (angle selection, ICP specificity, source completeness, brief template)
- [skl-tov.md](skl-tov.md) — SKL-TOV — Tone of Voice (voice, language rules, structure, persuasion, proprietary phrases, anti-patterns)
