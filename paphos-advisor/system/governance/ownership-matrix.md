# Ownership Matrix

This document defines who is responsible for each domain within the Paphos Advisor system. Ownership means accountability for quality, accuracy, review cadence, and decisions within that domain — not necessarily doing all the work personally.

**Current state:** All domains are owned by Jason while the team is in its early stage. As the team grows, ownership should be delegated to the roles defined in the "Future Owner" column.

## Delegation Guidelines

When handing ownership of a domain to a new team member or role, the outgoing owner should:

1. Brief the incoming owner on the domain's current state, open issues, and upcoming review dates.
2. Update this ownership matrix and commit the change to GitHub.
3. Reassign the relevant Notion records (Owner field) to the new owner.
4. Remain available for questions for a minimum of 30 days post-handover.

New owners are accountable from the date of handover, not from when they feel fully comfortable. If in doubt, escalate to the overall system owner (currently Jason).
