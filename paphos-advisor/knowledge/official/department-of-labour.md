## Purpose

This file captures official reference data from the Department of Labour relevant to non-EU work permits: salary thresholds by category, national minimum wage, labour market test requirements, Business Facilitation Unit (BFU) criteria, and ERGANI system compliance obligations.

All entries marked `[NEEDS VALIDATION]` have not been confirmed directly with the Department of Labour.

---

## National Minimum Wage (2026)

| Effective Date | Monthly Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| January 2026 | **€1,125/month** | Rose from €1,000/month. Work permit salary thresholds indexed proportionally. |

| Foreign interest company — key personnel | €2,500 | €30,000 | **Waived** | BFU-registered employers only |

**Note:** Thresholds are subject to annual review in line with minimum wage changes. Always confirm current thresholds at point of application.

## Labour Market Test

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Where to apply | District Labour Office serving the employer's area |
| Advertising period | Approximately **15 working days** |
| What is tested | No suitable EU/EEA/Swiss candidate available for the role |
| Exemptions | Foreign interest companies (BFU-registered); EU Blue Card applicants |
| Outcome | Written confirmation from Department of Labour; required for CRMD employer application |

## Foreign Interest Company (Business Facilitation Unit)

Companies registered with the BFU bypass the labour market test entirely — a significant advantage for non-EU employers hiring non-EU staff.

| Requirement | Detail | Validation Status |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum paid-up capital | €200,000 | [NEEDS VALIDATION — confirm current BFU requirements as of 2026] |
| Shareholding structure | Majority non-EU shareholding | [NEEDS VALIDATION] |
| Registration body | Business Facilitation Unit (BFU), Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry | |
| Benefit | Labour market test fully waived for key personnel and middle management | |

## ERGANI Electronic Employment System

All employment registrations and changes must flow through the ERGANI digital platform. Employer compliance is mandatory from day one of employment.

| Obligation | Deadline | Fine for Non-Compliance |
|---|---|---|
| New hire registration | **Within 1 day** of commencement | Up to €5,500 |
| Employer change notification (to CRMD) | Within 7 days | [NEEDS VALIDATION] |
| Annual compliance submission | **January–February** window each year | Up to €5,500 |

**Practical note:** Employers unfamiliar with ERGANI should work with their accountant or employment lawyer to ensure registration from day one. A delay of even one day constitutes a breach.

---

## Employer Change Rules (for Work Permit Holders)

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Employer changes 1–3 | Permitted with restrictions; each change must be approved and reported to CRMD |
| 4th employer change | Permit holder must **leave Cyprus for 2 years** before reapplying |
| Reporting requirement | All employer changes must be reported to CRMD within 7 days |

## Notes for Agents

- Salary thresholds rose in January 2026 following the minimum wage increase. Clients should not rely on figures from pre-2026 sources.
- BFU registration is a material advantage for companies planning to hire multiple non-EU staff. The €200,000 paid-up capital requirement means it's relevant for established operations, not startup registrations.
- ERGANI compliance is the employer's responsibility, not the permit holder's. If an employer fails to register, the employee is exposed despite having done nothing wrong. Agents should flag this risk to employer clients.
- The EU Blue Card (July 2025) is worth highlighting for clients hiring highly skilled workers — it processes in 90 days and grants EU-wide mobility, making Cyprus a more attractive base for international talent.
