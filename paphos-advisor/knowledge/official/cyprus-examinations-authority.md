## Purpose

This file captures reference data for the A2 Greek language examination and Civic Knowledge Test administered by the Cyprus Examinations Authority — both are mandatory requirements for Long-Term Resident (LTR) status applications. The confidence level on this file is **low** because most details come from secondary sources and have not been verified directly with the Authority.

This file should be prioritised for direct validation, as the A2 exam is the single biggest practical bottleneck for LTR applicants.

### Preparation Guidance

| Starting point | Estimated preparation time |
|---|---|
| No prior Greek knowledge | 3–6 months full-time study (estimate) |
| Some basic Greek | 1–3 months |
| A1 level Greek | 4–8 weeks |
| A2 already achieved | No preparation required — proceed directly to registration |

**Practical note for agents:** LTR applicants frequently underestimate the A2 requirement. The exam tests reading, writing, listening, and speaking at elementary level — it is not trivial for someone who has not actively studied Greek. Advise clients to begin preparation at least 12 months before their 5-year qualifying period ends. There are no exemptions, not even for long-term residents who communicate informally in Greek.

## CRMD Acceptance Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Certificate issuer | Cyprus Examinations Authority only |
| What CRMD will reject | Any A2 certificate from a language school, private tutor, or non-Cypriot authority |
| Submission | Certificate submitted as part of the MLT3 application pack |

**Important:** Language schools and private tutors can prepare applicants for the exam but cannot issue the qualifying certificate. Only the Cyprus Examinations Authority certificate satisfies CRMD.
