## Purpose

This file captures official reference data for CRMD-administered immigration permits: fees, form codes, income thresholds, health insurance requirements, processing times, and office contacts. Agents should use this file for factual lookups rather than relying on figures embedded in individual process docs.

All entries marked `[NEEDS VALIDATION]` have not been confirmed directly with CRMD and should not be stated as definitive to clients.

## Notes for Agents

- Paphos CRMD processing is slower than Nicosia for standard permits — quote 6–8 months for Pink Slip, not the 3–4 months some national guides suggest.
- September is the worst month to submit at any CRMD office. Students and employers should plan accordingly.
- Category F backlog is severe. Always advise clients to apply for Pink Slip and Category F simultaneously — Pink Slip provides legal residence while Category F processes.
- Form codes for several older permit categories (Pink Slip, Digital Nomad) are unconfirmed and should be validated with Paphos CRMD before client guidance is given.
