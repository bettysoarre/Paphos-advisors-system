# Transport FAQs

Covers driving licence exchange for foreign nationals relocating to Cyprus.

---

## Q: How long does it take to exchange a foreign driving licence in Cyprus?

**Short answer:** Appointments book 2–3 months in advance. Apply as soon as you arrive — do not wait until your foreign licence is about to expire.

Converting a foreign driving licence to a Cyprus licence requires an appointment at the Department of Road Transport. Appointment availability is typically 2–3 months out, and the process can take additional time if your issuing country needs to be contacted to verify the licence authenticity. UK nationals can exchange under the post-Brexit arrangements, but the verification process adds further time. EU and EEA licence holders generally have a more straightforward process. You can legally drive on your valid foreign licence while the exchange is in progress. To avoid any gap in driving entitlement, book your appointment as soon as you arrive in Cyprus — do not wait.
