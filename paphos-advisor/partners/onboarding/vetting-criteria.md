# Partner Vetting Criteria

## Purpose
Defines the minimum standards a potential partner must meet before being invited to join the Paphos Advisors partner network.

## Reference
Used in Stage 1 of the partner onboarding checklist and in SOP-PAR-002 (Partner Vetting SOP).

---

## Mandatory Criteria (all must be met)

### 1. Regulatory compliance
The partner must be appropriately registered/licensed for all services they offer:

| Partner category | Required registration |
|---|---|
| Immigration lawyer | Cyprus Bar Association |
| Tax advisor / accountant | ICPAC (Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Cyprus) |
| Property agent | CREAA (Cyprus Real Estate Agents Association) |
| Financial advisor | CySEC authorisation (if providing regulated investment advice) |
| Insurance broker | Insurance Companies Control Service (ICCS) |
| Healthcare provider | Cyprus Medical Council or relevant regulatory body |
| Removal / shipping | No specific licence required, but must have liability insurance |

Failure on regulatory compliance is an automatic rejection — no exceptions.

### 2. Operates in Paphos (or serves our client base)
The partner must primarily or significantly serve clients in Paphos district, or provide remote services to our client segments island-wide.

### 3. Experience with international clients
The partner must have demonstrable experience working with expatriates and international clients — not just domestic Cyprus clients. This can be evidenced by:
- International client testimonials or reviews
- Languages spoken (English essential)
- Track record with clients from our ICP segments

### 4. No active regulatory complaints
The partner must have no active regulatory complaints or formal disciplinary proceedings. Check publicly available complaint records where possible.

### 5. No conflict of interest
The partner must not:
- Offer a competing advisory service that overlaps with Paphos Advisors' core offering
- Be a partner of a direct competitor advisory

---

## Preferred Criteria (not mandatory, but increase priority)

- Speaks English fluently (essential) and at least one other language relevant to our ICPs (Russian, French, German are valuable)
- Has worked with us or our clients before with positive outcomes
- Has a professional online presence (website, LinkedIn)
- Is a firm rather than a sole practitioner (capacity resilience)
- Proactively stays current on regulatory changes in their field
- Has a clear, documented fee structure they are willing to share

---

## Scoring Model (for prioritisation when multiple candidates exist)

Score 1-3 on each dimension:

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client volume capacity | Small sole practitioner | Medium firm | Large firm or multiple practitioners |
| ICP segment alignment | Serves general public | Has some expat clients | Primarily serves expats / international clients |
| Language coverage | English only | English + 1 other | English + 2+ others |
| Online reputation | No reviews or negative | Moderate positive reviews | Strong positive reviews |
| Responsiveness (first contact) | >48hrs | 24-48hrs | <24hrs |

Total 12-15: High priority onboarding candidate
Total 8-11: Good candidate, proceed
Total 5-7: Proceed cautiously — gaps to address
Total <5: Consider deferring until gaps are addressed

---

## Rejection Records
All rejection decisions must be logged in the Notion Research Log with the reason. Do not silently discard candidates — they may be suitable in future.
