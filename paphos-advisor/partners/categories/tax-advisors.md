# Partner Category: Tax Advisors

## Category ID
`tax-advisors`

## Description
Qualified accountants and tax advisors registered with ICPAC (Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Cyprus) who provide personal and corporate tax advisory services to individuals relocating to Cyprus.

## Regulatory requirement
Registration with **ICPAC** is mandatory for practising accountants in Cyprus. Verify registration before onboarding.

## Services relevant to our clients

| Service | Relevant ICP segments |
|---|---|
| Cyprus tax residency setup (60-Day Rule) | EU Remote Worker, Entrepreneur |
| Non-Dom status application | EU Remote Worker, HNI, Entrepreneur |
| Personal tax return filing | All tax residents |
| Tax planning and structuring | HNI, Entrepreneur |
| Double taxation treaty advice | All with foreign income |
| VAT registration (individual) | Entrepreneurs |
| GESY registration | All employed/self-employed |

## Knowledge interview focus areas

When interviewing a tax advisor, prioritise:
- Practical steps to establish 60-day rule residency — what the tax office actually asks for
- Non-Dom clock start date — when does Year 1 begin exactly?
- GHS nuances for different client types (employed, self-employed, director)
- Common mistakes clients make that create tax problems
- Recent legislative changes and their practical impact

## Referral criteria

Refer a client to a tax advisor when:
- The client is seeking to establish Cyprus tax residency
- The client wants to benefit from Non-Dom status
- The client has complex income (multiple sources, foreign pensions, investment income)
- The client needs help filing a Cyprus personal tax return
- The client is setting up a Cyprus company and needs tax structuring advice
