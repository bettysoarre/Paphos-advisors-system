## Knowledge interview focus areas

When interviewing an immigration lawyer, prioritise:
- Real-world processing times at Paphos CRMD
- Documentation requirements beyond the official list
- Common application rejection reasons and how to avoid them
- Any recent changes in office behaviour or requirements

## Referral criteria

Refer a client to an immigration lawyer when:
- The client needs MEU1 and prefers to have professional handling rather than DIY
- The client has a complex situation (dual nationality, prior visa issues, non-standard documents)
- The client needs any permit beyond the basic Yellow Slip
- The client has received a rejection or refusal and needs to appeal

Do NOT refer simply because the process exists — many clients can handle the Yellow Slip themselves with our guidance.
