# Partner Category: Removal / Shipping Companies

## Category ID
`removal-companies`

## Description
International removal and shipping companies that handle the transportation of household goods to Cyprus, including customs clearance.

## Regulatory requirement
No specific Cyprus licence required. Must have public liability insurance. Membership of an industry body (BAR — British Association of Removers, FIDI, IAM) is a strong positive indicator.

## Services relevant to our clients

| Service | Relevant ICP segments |
|---|---|
| Full household goods removal to Cyprus | UK Retiree, Families, EU Remote Worker |
| Partial/groupage shipment | Remote workers with fewer goods |
| Customs clearance (EU goods) | EU nationals |
| Customs clearance (non-EU goods) | UK nationals, non-EU nationals |
| Storage (pre-move or on arrival) | All |
| Pet transport | Families |
| Vehicle transport | Any client importing a vehicle |

## Knowledge interview focus areas

When interviewing a removal company, prioritise:
- What documents are required for customs clearance in Cyprus?
- Are there differences for EU vs non-EU origin goods?
- What duty-free allowances apply for new residents?
- What are the common delays or problems at Limassol port?
- How far in advance should clients book?
- What is typically NOT worth shipping (better to buy new in Cyprus)?

## Referral criteria

Refer a client to a removal company when:
- The client is shipping household goods from their country of origin
- The client needs customs clearance advice
- The client is importing a vehicle (and wants a specialist who can also manage the shipping)

## Notes
Most UK-based removal companies handle Cyprus as a standard destination. The key differentiator is their knowledge of Cyprus customs procedures and whether they have a reliable local agent in Cyprus. EU goods (post-Brexit, UK is non-EU) have different procedures from EU national shipments. Always clarify the nationality/origin of the shipment when routing.
