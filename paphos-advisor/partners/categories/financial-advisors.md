# Partner Category: Financial Advisors

## Category ID
`financial-advisors`

## Description
Regulated financial planners and advisors who work with expats on investments, pensions, protection, and wealth planning in the context of a Cyprus relocation.

## Regulatory requirement
Advisors providing regulated investment advice must be authorised by **CySEC** (Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission) or be passporting their authorisation from another EU jurisdiction. Verify before onboarding.

## Services relevant to our clients

| Service | Relevant ICP segments |
|---|---|
| Pension transfer planning (QROPS/SIPP) | UK Retiree |
| Investment planning and portfolio management | HNI, UK Retiree |
| Life insurance and protection planning | All |
| Wealth structuring for relocation | HNI |
| Offshore savings account guidance | EU Remote Worker, HNI |
| Currency exchange services | All (at point of relocation) |

## Process docs they inform

Financial advisors primarily inform knowledge articles rather than process documents, as their work is less procedural and more advisory.

## Knowledge interview focus areas

When interviewing a financial advisor, prioritise:
- What pension planning issues are most common for UK nationals relocating to Cyprus?
- What are the QROPS requirements and is Cyprus still a qualifying jurisdiction?
- What tax implications should clients understand before moving pensions or investments?
- What does the typical HNI wealth structuring engagement look like in the context of a Cyprus move?
- What are the currency exchange considerations for clients moving capital to Cyprus?

## Referral criteria

Refer a client to a financial advisor when:
- A UK national client has pension assets they are considering transferring
- A client has significant investment assets and needs advice on restructuring for Cyprus residency
- A client needs life insurance or protection planning
- A client is managing significant currency conversion as part of their move

## Notes
Financial advisors are a sensitive category. Ensure any referred advisor is genuinely regulated and authorised for the specific services they provide. The UK pension transfer market in particular has historically been targeted by unscrupulous operators. Due diligence before onboarding is essential.

Do not refer clients to financial advisors for tax advice — that is the domain of tax advisors (ICPAC registered). The boundary: financial advisors advise on investments and protection; tax advisors advise on Cyprus tax obligations.
