# Partner Category: Healthcare Providers

## Category ID
`healthcare-providers`

## Description
Private clinics, hospitals, and specialist practices in Paphos that serve English-speaking expat clients alongside GESY patients.

## Regulatory requirement
Registered with the **Cyprus Medical Council** (doctors) and/or the **Ministry of Health** (clinics and hospitals). Verify registration before onboarding.

## Services relevant to our clients

| Service | Relevant ICP segments |
|---|---|
| GP / family medicine (English-speaking) | All, especially UK Retiree and Families |
| Private health check / medical certificate | UK Retiree, Non-EU nationals (for permit applications) |
| Specialist referral | UK Retiree, HNI |
| GESY-registered practice | All registered GESY patients |
| Paediatrics | Families |
| Dental | All |

## Knowledge interview focus areas

When interviewing a healthcare provider, prioritise:
- What does the GESY registration process look like from the patient side?
- What are the practical differences between GESY and private in terms of access and waiting times?
- What medical certificate is required for Category F permit applicants?
- What health insurance policies are typically accepted by the CRMD for permit applications?
- What are the most common health concerns for the expat population they serve?

## Referral criteria

Refer a client to a healthcare provider when:
- The client needs a GP and does not know how to find one in Paphos
- The client needs a medical certificate for a permit application
- The client needs GESY card activation / first GP registration
- The client needs specialist care that GESY waiting times cannot accommodate

## Notes
GESY-registered GPs are needed by all Cyprus residents — this is a universal settling-in need. The expat market particularly values English-speaking GPs. The best healthcare partners for us are practices that serve both GESY and private patients, understand the expat experience, and can provide medicals for permit applications.
