# Partner Category: Property Agents

## Category ID
`property-agents`

## Description
Licensed property agents registered with CREAA (Cyprus Real Estate Agents Association) who assist clients with renting and/or purchasing property in Paphos and surrounding areas.

## Regulatory requirement
Registration with **CREAA** is mandatory for real estate agents in Cyprus. Verify registration before onboarding.

## Services relevant to our clients

| Service | Relevant ICP segments |
|---|---|
| Rental search (long-term, 12+ months) | EU Remote Worker, UK Retiree, Families |

## Knowledge interview focus areas

When interviewing a property agent, prioritise:
- Current rental market: typical prices, availability, and landlord requirements in Paphos
- Title deed situation: what % of property in Paphos has clean title? What are the risks?
- Stamp-duty abolition impact: are landlords still getting contracts stamped at the Tax Office?
- What documents do landlords typically require from prospective tenants?
- Timeline from offer to completion for a typical purchase
- Areas of Paphos that suit different client types (families, retirees, professionals)

## Referral criteria

Refer a client to a property agent when:
- The client needs to find a long-term rental (especially for MEU1 qualification)
- The client is ready to buy property
- The client needs area guidance specific to their family situation or lifestyle

## Notes
Property agents in Cyprus often specialise in either rentals or sales, or in specific areas (Paphos town, Peyia, Chloraka, Coral Bay, etc.). Know which agents cover which areas when routing. For HNI permanent residency clients, the €300k threshold means they need an agent familiar with off-plan and developer projects.
