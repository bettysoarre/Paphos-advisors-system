# Referral Eligibility Rules

## Purpose
Defines when a partner is eligible to receive referrals from Paphos Advisors.

---

## Partner Eligibility

A partner may receive referrals only when they meet ALL of the following:

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Status | `active` only — not `probationary`, `onboarded`, `suspended`, or `archived` |
| Trust level | `trusted` or above |
| Regulatory compliance | Confirmed and current |
| Contract on file | Yes |
| Knowledge interview | Completed |
| 90-day review | Completed (for partners who started as `probationary`) |

**Exception:** In exceptional circumstances (e.g., an urgent client need and no `trusted` partner available in that category), a `probationary` partner may receive a referral with explicit disclosure to the client and Jason's approval. This must be logged in the case notes.

---

## Service-Level Eligibility

A partner is only eligible to receive referrals for services they have confirmed they provide. Check the `Services Offered` field in the Notion Partners database before making a referral.

Do not refer a client to a partner for a service that:
- Is outside the partner's confirmed service list
- Is outside their regulatory scope
- The partner has indicated they are at capacity for

---

## Client Eligibility

A client referral is eligible only when:
- The client has confirmed they want to be referred
- The client has been informed of the referral relationship and any commercial arrangement (disclosure requirement — see `referral-disclosure-requirements.md`)
- The client's situation matches the partner's expertise (e.g., do not refer a non-EU national to a partner who only handles EU national immigration)

---

## Capacity Check

Before making a referral, confirm the partner has capacity. If a partner has indicated they are busy or at capacity, do not make referrals until they confirm availability. Check the most recent contact with the partner.

---

## Exclusions

Do not refer to a partner when:
- The partner has an active quality issue or complaint under investigation
- The partner has been suspended
- The client has specifically asked not to be referred to a particular firm
- The referral would create a conflict of interest (e.g., the partner also represents the other party in a property transaction)
