# Referral Disclosure Requirements

## Purpose
Defines what must be disclosed to clients before a referral is made, and how to disclose it. These requirements protect the client and protect Paphos Advisors.

---

## The Principle

Clients must always know:
1. That we are making a referral
2. Whether we have a commercial relationship with the partner (fee-sharing, reciprocal referrals)
3. That they are free to choose their own professional

We do not make undisclosed referrals. We do not steer clients based on our commercial benefit rather than their best interest.

---

## Disclosure Script

Before making any referral, say or write the following (adapt as appropriate to the situation):

> "We work with a network of vetted professionals in Cyprus who we've personally assessed. I'd like to introduce you to [partner category description — e.g., 'an immigration lawyer we work with']. We have a referral relationship with them, which means [if applicable: we may receive a referral fee / they refer clients to us / we have a reciprocal arrangement]. You're not obligated to use them — if you'd prefer to find your own advisor, that's absolutely fine. But we've found them to be reliable and knowledgeable."

If there is NO commercial arrangement:
> "I'd like to introduce you to [partner]. We have no financial relationship with them — we recommend them because we've found their work to be of high quality and relevant to your situation."

---

## Written Disclosure

For email introductions, include this paragraph:

> *Note: Paphos Advisors has a referral relationship with [partner name/firm]. [If fee arrangement: We may receive a referral fee for this introduction. / If reciprocal: They occasionally refer clients to us in return.] This does not affect the advice we give you, and you are not obligated to use their services.*

---

## What Must Be Disclosed

| Relationship type | Disclosure required |
|---|---|
| No commercial arrangement | Confirm this explicitly ("we have no financial arrangement") |
| Referral fee received | Disclose existence of fee (exact amount not required) |
| Reciprocal referrals | Disclose the reciprocal arrangement |
| Partner is also a client of ours | Disclose the dual relationship |

---

## Website Disclosure

The following notice must appear on any website page that includes partner profiles or referral callouts:

> *Paphos Advisors works with a network of vetted professionals. Where we refer you to a third party, we may have a referral arrangement in place. We only refer to professionals we have assessed ourselves, but we encourage you to conduct your own due diligence. Referrals do not affect the independence of our advice.*

---

## Record-Keeping

For every referral made, log in the Case notes:
- Date of referral
- Partner referred to
- Whether disclosure was given
- Client's response

If a client declines a referral, note this. Do not pressure.
