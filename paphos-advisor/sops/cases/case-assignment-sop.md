# Case Assignment SOP

## Purpose
Defines how active cases are assigned to team members and how workload is managed.

## Current Context
With a single advisor (Jason), all cases are assigned to Jason by default. This SOP is structured for when the team grows.

---

## Assignment Criteria

Cases should be assigned based on:

1. **Process expertise** — does the team member have knowledge of the processes this case requires?
2. **Current workload** — how many active cases does each team member currently hold?
3. **ICP familiarity** — has the team member worked with this ICP segment before?
4. **Availability** — given the client's timeline and urgency

---

## Steps

### 1. Review the case at intake
When a new case is confirmed (status changes to `Active`), review the processes required and assess which team member is best placed.

### 2. Assign in Notion
Set the `Assigned Advisor` field in the Case record.

### 3. Handover briefing
If assigning to someone other than the person who handled intake:
- Conduct a brief verbal handover (10-15 minutes)
- Ensure the assignee has read the case notes and understands the client's situation
- Confirm the next action and due date

### 4. Client notification
If the client will be working with a different person from their initial contact, introduce the assigned advisor by email.

## Reassignment
If a case needs to be reassigned mid-engagement (illness, capacity, expertise gap):
1. Update the `Assigned Advisor` field in Notion
2. Brief the new assignee
3. Notify the client
4. Do not leave a case without an assigned advisor for more than 24 hours
