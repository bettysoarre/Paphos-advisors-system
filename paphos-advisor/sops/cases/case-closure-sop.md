# Case Closure SOP

## Purpose
Defines the steps to close a case cleanly, capture learnings, and maintain the client relationship for future engagement.

## When to Use
When all deliverables for a case have been completed, or when a client has decided not to proceed.

---

## Closure Types

**Closed — Won:** Case completed successfully. Client achieved what they came for.
**Closed — Lost:** Client did not proceed (changed mind, chose competitor, circumstances changed).

---

## Steps for Closed — Won

### 1. Confirm all deliverables are complete
- Review the original scope in the Case notes
- Confirm each item has been delivered
- Confirm client has received everything they need

### 2. Final client communication
Send a closing message:
- Summarise what was achieved
- Provide any final documents or references they should keep
- Note any upcoming actions that are now their responsibility (e.g., annual tax returns, Pink Slip application after 5 years)
- Invite them to come back if they need further help

### 3. Request a review (where appropriate)
If the client had a positive experience, this is the right moment to ask for a Google review or testimonial. Do not ask before the case is fully resolved.

### 4. Update Notion
- Set Case status to `Closed — Won`
- Set Date Closed
- Record final notes in Case Notes
- Confirm all partner referrals are logged

### 5. Contact record maintenance
Update the Contact record:
- Set status to `Past Client`
- Note any future needs they mentioned (e.g., "Will need Pink Slip in 4 years")
- Set a reminder for future outreach if appropriate

### 6. Capture learnings
For cases that involved unusual complexity or a knowledge gap:
- Was any new information captured that should update process docs?
- Did a partner perform exceptionally or poorly?
- Was there a question the client asked that we could not easily answer? (potential content or KB article)

Log any learnings in the Research Log or raise a content brief.

---

## Steps for Closed — Lost

### 1. Note the reason
Record in Notion why the client did not proceed. Categories:
- Price / budget
- Chose competitor
- Changed plans
- Process too complex
- No response / ghost

This data informs future ICP and service decisions.

### 2. Final communication
For clients who actively chose not to proceed: send a brief, gracious message. Leave the door open. Do not pressure.

### 3. Update Notion
- Set Case status to `Closed — Lost`
- Set Date Closed
- Note reason in Case Notes

### 4. Contact record
Set Contact status to `Past Client` (not `Not a Fit` unless that is accurate).
