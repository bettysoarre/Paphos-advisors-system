# Case Intake SOP

## Purpose
Defines how to handle a new client enquiry from first contact through to a confirmed engagement.

---

## Steps

### 1. Initial contact response
Respond to all new enquiries within 1 business day. Acknowledge receipt and set expectations for when a full response will follow.

### 2. Create a Contact record
In Notion Contacts database:
- Full name, email, phone
- How they found us
- Referring partner (if applicable)
- First contact date
- GDPR consent confirmed

### 3. Initial needs assessment
In the first substantive interaction, identify:
- What is their current situation (nationality, location, family situation)?
- What are they trying to achieve in Cyprus?
- What is their timeline?
- Have they already taken any steps?

Use this to:
- Identify the likely ICP segment
- Identify the relevant processes
- Assess whether this is a case we can help with

### 4. Fit assessment
Assess whether this client is a good fit:
- Do the processes they need fall within our service scope?
- Is their timeline realistic given current processing times?
- Is their budget aligned with the complexity of their case?

If not a fit:
- Be honest with the client — do not take a case you cannot serve well
- Where possible, point them to a more appropriate resource
- Set status to `Not a Fit` in Notion with a reason noted

### 5. Create a Case record
If the client is a fit:
- Create a Case record in Notion linked to the Contact
- Set status to `Assessment`
- Assign ICP segment
- Tag the relevant processes required
- Note the initial assessment in Case Notes

### 6. Proposal
Prepare a clear scope of services:
- What we will do
- What we will not do (important for managing expectations)
- Timeline estimate
- Fee

Send the proposal and set status to `Proposal Sent`.

### 7. Confirm engagement
When the client confirms:
- Set Case status to `Active`
- Confirm fee arrangement
- Set next action and due date

---

## Notes on GDPR
Before storing any personal information, confirm GDPR consent is recorded. This is non-negotiable. If a client refuses consent, we cannot maintain their records in our system and cannot take the case.
