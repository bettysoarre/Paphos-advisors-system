# Case Escalation SOP

## Purpose
Defines how to handle cases that go beyond normal scope — whether due to complexity, urgency, a client complaint, or a process exception.

---

## Escalation Triggers

| Trigger | Type | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Process is outside our documented scope | Complexity | Refer to specialist; see below |
| Client is in an urgent situation (visa expiry, court deadline) | Urgency | Immediate senior review |
| Client has received incorrect advice previously | Complexity | Document and involve a specialist |
| Client is unhappy with service | Complaint | Follow complaint process below |
| A partner has made an error affecting a client | Partner failure | Follow partner review + client remediation |
| A regulatory change affects an active case mid-process | Regulatory | Review and advise immediately |
| Conflicting official/field guidance creates ambiguity | Knowledge gap | Log knowledge gap; get specialist input |

---

## Escalation to a Specialist

When a case requires expertise beyond our current scope:

1. Identify the appropriate specialist (immigration lawyer, tax advisor, etc.)
2. Brief the client that you are bringing in specialist input
3. Make a warm introduction to the specialist partner
4. Remain involved as the client's point of contact — do not abandon the client to the specialist
5. Log the escalation in the Case notes with date and reason

---

## Urgency Protocol

If a client has a hard deadline (visa expiry, property completion, tax filing):

1. Mark the case `Priority: High` in Notion
2. Set a next action date of today or tomorrow
3. Contact all relevant parties (client, partner, authority) immediately
4. Update the client daily until the urgency is resolved

---

## Complaint Handling

If a client raises a formal complaint:

1. Acknowledge the complaint within 24 hours — in writing
2. Do not become defensive. Listen and understand the issue first
3. Investigate: what happened, what was communicated, where the expectation divergence occurred
4. Respond with findings within 5 business days
5. If we made an error: acknowledge it clearly, apologise, and correct it
6. If the expectation was set incorrectly: explain what was in scope and agree a resolution
7. Log the complaint and resolution in the Case notes
8. Review whether the complaint reveals a systemic issue that needs a process change

---

## Knowledge Gap Protocol

If a case reveals that our documentation is incomplete or incorrect:

1. Note the gap in the Case notes
2. Do not guess or improvise — tell the client you are confirming before advising
3. Run a research session or consult a partner to get the right answer
4. Update the relevant process doc or KB article
5. Log the research session in the Research Log

The case is paused on this point until the knowledge gap is filled. Do not advise on unresolved uncertainty.
