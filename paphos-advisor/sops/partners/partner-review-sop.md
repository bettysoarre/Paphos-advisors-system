# Partner Review SOP

## Purpose
Defines the process for reviewing active partners on a scheduled cadence to maintain quality standards.

## Cadence
- **Probationary partners:** 90-day review after activation
- **Trusted partners:** Annual review
- **Triggered review:** Any time a client raises a quality issue with a referred partner

---

## Scheduled Review Steps

### 1. Pull the partner record from Notion
Review:
- Number of referrals made in the review period
- Client outcomes where known
- Any complaints or issues logged
- Whether their knowledge has been updated (check last interview date)

### 2. Assess client outcomes
For referrals made in the review period:
- Did clients report positive experiences? (Check case notes)
- Were there any delays, miscommunications, or quality failures?
- Did the partner communicate proactively or did we have to chase?

### 3. Assess knowledge currency
- Has the partner's practice area had regulatory changes since the last interview?
- If yes, is the partner's guidance still current?
- Consider whether a refresh interview is warranted

### 4. Assess commercial relationship
- Have referrals been reciprocated (if that was part of the arrangement)?
- Are commercial terms still appropriate?

### 5. Trust level decision
| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| Strong performance, probationary | Upgrade to `trusted` |
| Satisfactory, no issues | Retain current status |
| Minor issues, first occurrence | Flag in notes, retain status, discuss with partner |
| Recurring issues or quality failure | Demote to `probationary` or `suspended` |
| Regulatory issue or client harm | Immediately set to `suspended`, escalate |

### 6. Update Notion
- Update trust level if changed
- Log the review date and outcome in the Notes field
- Set next review date

### 7. Partner communication
For any status change, communicate with the partner directly before the change takes effect. Never demote a partner silently.

---

## Triggered Review (Quality Issue)

If a client raises a quality concern about a referred partner:
1. Log the issue in the relevant Case notes immediately
2. Assess severity: minor (misunderstanding) vs material (wrong advice, delay, misconduct)
3. For material issues: immediately suspend referrals to that partner pending review
4. Run the scheduled review steps above with focus on the specific issue
5. Decide: retain, probation, suspend, or remove
6. If the issue was misconduct: consider whether a regulatory complaint is appropriate
