# Partner Vetting SOP

## Purpose
Defines how to assess whether a potential partner meets the criteria to join the Paphos Advisors partner network.

## Reference
Full vetting criteria are in `partners/onboarding/vetting-criteria.md`.

---

## Steps

### 1. Regulatory compliance check
Before any other assessment, verify:
- Is the partner regulated / licensed for the services they provide?
- For lawyers: confirm registration with the Cyprus Bar Association
- For tax advisors: confirm registration with ICPAC (Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Cyprus)
- For property agents: confirm registration with CREAA (Cyprus Real Estate Agents Association)
- For financial advisors: confirm CySEC authorisation (if providing regulated financial services)

**If unregulated for regulated activities: do not proceed.**

### 2. Online presence review
Assess:
- Do they have a professional website?
- Are there reviews on Google, LinkedIn, or expat forums? What do they say?
- Are there any regulatory complaints or news articles indicating poor practice?
- Is their listed area of practice consistent with what we need?

### 3. Market positioning assessment
Assess whether they serve our client base:
- Do they work with expatriates and international clients?
- Do they operate in Paphos (or the relevant geography)?
- Are their fee levels appropriate for our client segments?

### 4. Capacity check
Assess whether they can handle referrals:
- Are they a sole practitioner or a firm? Can they absorb additional client load?
- Do they have a track record of timely service (from reviews or direct intel)?

### 5. Conflict of interest check
Assess:
- Do they have a competing advisory offering that would conflict with our own?
- Are they already a partner of a direct competitor?

### 6. Initial quality assessment
From information available pre-contact:
- Does their public guidance (website, LinkedIn articles) appear accurate and up to date?
- Do they cite current regulations and correct procedure?

### 7. Decision

**Proceed** — candidate meets criteria. Move to First Contact (Stage 2 of onboarding).
**Hold** — candidate has potential but has gaps (e.g. no visible reviews, uncertain capacity). Note the gap and revisit in 3 months.
**Reject** — candidate fails on regulatory compliance, quality, or conflict of interest grounds. Log the rejection in Notion Research Log with reason.
