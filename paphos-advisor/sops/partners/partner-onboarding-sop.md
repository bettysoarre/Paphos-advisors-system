# Partner Onboarding SOP

## Purpose
Defines the full process for identifying, vetting, interviewing, and activating a new partner. This SOP governs how Paphos Advisors builds its partner network.

## Reference
This SOP is operationalised through the checklist at `partners/onboarding/partner-onboarding-checklist.md`.

---

## Overview

Partner onboarding follows 7 stages:

1. Initial Assessment
2. First Contact
3. Information Gathering
4. Formal Interview
5. Knowledge Extraction
6. Record Completion
7. Activation

Full details for each stage are in the partner onboarding checklist. This SOP provides the governing principles.

---

## Governing Principles

### Quality over quantity
We do not onboard partners simply to have a large network. Each partner must meet the vetting criteria in `partners/onboarding/vetting-criteria.md`. A poor partner referral damages client trust more than having no referral at all.

### Knowledge extraction is mandatory
The formal interview (Stage 4) is not optional. It is the primary mechanism for acquiring field intelligence. A partner who refuses to be interviewed cannot be an active partner. They can remain as `onboarded` but not progress to `active`.

### Trust levels are earned
All new partners start at `probationary` trust level. They move to `trusted` after:
- 3+ months as active partner
- At least 2 referrals with positive client outcomes
- No material complaints or quality issues
- 90-day review completed satisfactorily

### Commercial terms must be documented
Before a partner is activated, commercial terms (if any) must be in writing and Contract on File must be marked `Yes` in Notion.

### Partners are not clients
Our obligation is to our clients. If a partner repeatedly delivers poor outcomes for referred clients, they are demoted or removed regardless of the commercial relationship.

## Partner Categories

Partners are classified by category. See `partners/categories/` for full definitions.

Current categories:
- Immigration Lawyers
- Tax Advisors
- Property Agents
- Removal / Shipping Companies
- Healthcare Providers
- Financial Advisors
- Insurance Brokers
- Education Consultants
- Banking / Financial Services

---

## Timing

There is no fixed timeline for onboarding. A partner moves through stages when the work is done, not on a clock. However:
- Stage 1–2: Should be completed within 1 week of identifying a partner candidate
- Stage 3–4: Should be completed within 3 weeks of first contact
- Stage 5–6: Should be completed within 1 week of the interview
- Stage 7: Activation on the same day as record completion

---

## Records

All partner records live in the Notion Partners database. The GitHub repository holds:
- Partner category definitions (`partners/categories/`)
- Onboarding checklist and vetting criteria (`partners/onboarding/`)
- Partner profile template (`partners/_templates/partner-profile-template.md`)
- Referral rules (`partners/referral-rules/`)

Field intelligence from interviews is stored in `knowledge/field/` and linked to process documents.
