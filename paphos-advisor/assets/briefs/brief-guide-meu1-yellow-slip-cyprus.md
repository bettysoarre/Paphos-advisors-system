## Objective

Help an EU national who has decided to relocate to Cyprus apply for their MEU1 Yellow Slip without avoidable errors or rejections — covering documents, stamping requirements, real-world processing times, and what to do when things go wrong — so they can complete this first critical step and unlock banking, tax residency, and healthcare registration.

## Key Points to Cover

**Must include:**
- What the Yellow Slip is: the registration certificate confirming an EU/EEA/Swiss national's right to reside in Cyprus for more than 3 months. Does not expire.
- EU/EEA/Swiss nationals only — not UK nationals post-Brexit (link to Category F article)
- The 4 applicant categories: employed, self-employed, self-sufficient, student
- Remote workers apply as self-sufficient — define what self-sufficient means and what it requires
- Documents list: MEU1 form, passport, 2 photos, proof of address, financial proof, health insurance, €20 cash
- **The rental agreement stamping requirement** — this is the single most common rejection trigger. The agreement must be stamped at the Tax Department before attending CRMD. Minimum 12-month term required.
- Financial resources benchmark: €1,000–€1,500/month net is the practitioner-cited figure. Not an officially published threshold — label it clearly as a practitioner benchmark.
- Where to apply: CRMD district office in person. Cannot send a representative.
- Real-world processing times by district: Paphos 1–3 months (vs. official "5 days")
- What happens if you are given a receipt and told to wait — this is normal in Paphos
- What the Yellow Slip unlocks: banking, tax residency, GESY registration, vehicle registration

**Out of scope / avoid:**
- UK nationals and Category F Permit — separate article; mention exists, link out
- MEU2 (non-EU family members) — mention exists, link out, do not explain process
- GESY registration detail — mention it follows MEU1, link to GESY article when live
- Non-dom status and tax residency detail — mention the Yellow Slip supports it, link out
- Category 6.2 permanent residency — different segment entirely, do not reference

## Structure / Outline

### What is the MEU1 Yellow Slip and who needs one? (~200 words)
Define it clearly. EU/EEA/Swiss nationals only. Required for stays over 3 months. Does not expire. One paragraph signposting UK nationals to Category F article.

### Which category applies to you? (~200 words)
The 4 categories: employed, self-employed, self-sufficient, student. For remote workers: self-sufficient is the standard route. Define self-sufficient: independent income that supports you without relying on Cypriot social assistance, plus health insurance valid in Cyprus.

### Documents you need — and the one requirement everyone misses (~400 words)
#### The rental agreement: what must be true before you go to CRMD
12-month minimum term. Must be stamped at the Tax Department BEFORE attending CRMD. Step-by-step: sign lease → take to Tax Department → get it stamped → then go to CRMD. Clearly label as the most common rejection cause.
#### Bank statements and the financial benchmark
6 months of statements. The €1,000–€1,500/month practitioner benchmark — label as practitioner guidance, not official threshold.
#### Health insurance
Private health insurance required for self-sufficient applicants. GESY may substitute if employed and contributing — flag as field intelligence, not confirmed.
#### Complete document checklist (table)
Document / Format / Notes — covers all items.

### Where to apply and how to prepare (~200 words)
CRMD district office in person — cannot use a representative for the initial application. Brief table of district offices. Call ahead in Paphos and Limassol. Bring originals + photocopies + 2 photos + €20 cash + completed MEU1 form. Download form in advance.

### How long does it actually take? (~250 words)
Official: same day or 5 working days. Reality table by district: Nicosia/Larnaca 2–4 weeks; Limassol 4–8 weeks; Paphos 1–3 months. Explain why the Paphos office is slower (understaffing, volume). What to do if you receive a receipt and are told to wait — keep it, follow up after the expected window. Government liaison option for Paphos.

### What your Yellow Slip unlocks (~100 words)
Brief list: Cyprus bank account, GESY registration, tax residency registration (60-day rule), vehicle registration. Link to each relevant guide where available.

### Common problems and how to fix them (~250 words)
Based on PROC-IMM-001 Common Issues:
- Rental agreement not stamped → fix: stamp at Tax Department, re-attend
- Bank statements rejected → fix: 6 months, both income and expenditure, in English
- Rental agreement under 12 months → fix: cannot use for MEU1; renegotiate or bridge
- Certificate not issued on the day → fix: normal in Paphos; keep receipt; follow up
- Missing documents at desk → fix: bring more than needed; check list with CRMD in advance

### Frequently asked questions (~200 words)
6 questions from ICP-SEG-002 Primary Concerns + sitemap column R:
- What is the MEU1 Yellow Slip and who needs to apply?
- What documents do I need for the Yellow Slip in Cyprus?
- How long does the Yellow Slip take in Paphos?
- Do I need a lawyer to apply for the Yellow Slip?
- Can I use a utility bill instead of a rental agreement?
- What happens if my documents are rejected at CRMD?
