## Objective

Help a founder or business owner understand the complete process for forming a Cyprus Private Limited Company (Ltd) — the steps, realistic timeline, costs, and what happens after incorporation — so they can instruct a Cyprus lawyer with confidence and set realistic expectations on banking timelines.

---

## Target Audience

- **ICP:** ICP-SEG-004 — Entrepreneur / Company Formation
- **Journey stage:** Ready to act — has decided Cyprus is the right jurisdiction; now researching the how
- **What they know:** High financial literacy; already aware of Cyprus's 12.5% corporate rate; has likely compared jurisdictions (Cyprus vs Malta is a common pre-arrival research path); understands basic company structures
- **What they are anxious about:** How long the bank account will take (they have heard it is slow); whether they need to be personally resident; what happens if the bank account application is rejected
- **Objections to address:** "Do I need to be in Cyprus in person?" / "How long does banking really take?" / "What are the substance requirements?"

## Key Points to Cover

**Must include:**
- Overview of the Cyprus Private Limited Company (HE company): why it is used, the key tax advantages (12.5% corporate rate, IP Box effective rate, non-dom dividend exemption for Cyprus-resident directors)
- Eligibility: any nationality, any country — no restrictions on directors or shareholders; minimum 1 director, 1 shareholder, a company secretary (Cyprus-resident), and a registered office
- The 9 steps: name check → structure → lawyer prepares and files → Registrar processes → receive certificates → TIC registration → VAT registration (if applicable) → corporate bank account → auditor/accounting setup
- The bank account as the long pole: company registers in 5–10 days (standard) or 1–3 days (expedited); bank account takes 4–12 weeks or longer. Total process from instruction to functioning account: 2–3 months
- EMI (Wise Business / Revolut Business) as an interim option — flag that acceptability varies and an accountant should confirm
- Documents required: certified passports + proof of address for all directors/shareholders; M&AA; HE1, HE2, HE3 forms; consent letters; subscriber declaration
- Fees: government registration ~€105–€400 (share capital dependent); expedited +€300–€500; lawyer/agent fees €500–€2,000+; annual registered office ~€300–€600; annual company secretary ~€300–€600
- Tax substance: board decisions should be made in Cyprus for the company to be Cyprus tax resident; at least one Cyprus-resident director is advisable; discuss with tax advisor before finalising structure
- Connection to 60-day rule: company directorship is the most common method to satisfy the Cyprus business activity condition
- Ongoing obligations: annual return (HE32), audited financial statements, corporate tax return (TD.4)

**Out of scope / avoid:**
- Public limited companies (PLCs)
- Branch offices of foreign companies
- VAT registration detail beyond the threshold mention
- IP Box detail — mention it exists; link to a future dedicated article
- Personal immigration detail — link to Yellow Slip or Category F articles as appropriate

## Structure / Outline

### Why entrepreneurs form Cyprus companies (~200 words)
Brief but punchy: 12.5% corporate rate; IP Box at effective 2.5% for qualifying IP income; non-dom dividend exemption when director is Cyprus tax resident. Position the company as part of a structure, not a standalone entity. Link to 60-day rule and non-dom articles. Do not over-explain the tax — that is for the tax-specific articles.

### Who can form a Cyprus company? (~150 words)
Any nationality, any residency — no restrictions. Minimum 1 director (any nationality, can be non-resident), 1 shareholder (can be corporate), Cyprus-resident company secretary (or licensed service provider), registered Cyprus office address. Brief mention of corporate purchase (foreign company forming a Cyprus subsidiary).

### The formation process — step by step (~600 words)
Follow PROC-BIZ-001 Steps 1–9 in sequence. For each step: what happens, who does it, how long it takes.

  #### Step 1: Name check (1–3 days)
  #### Step 2: Define structure — directors, shareholders, share capital, objects
  Tax substance note: board decisions in Cyprus; Cyprus-resident director advisable.
  #### Step 3: Lawyer prepares and files documents (2–5 days prep + filing)
  #### Step 4: Registrar processes (5–10 days standard / 1–3 days expedited)
  #### Step 5: Receive certificate of incorporation and company documents
  #### Step 6: Tax registration — TIC
  #### Step 7: VAT registration (if applicable)
  #### Step 8: Corporate bank account — the long pole (~200 words within section)
  Be explicit: this takes 4–12 weeks minimum, often longer. KYC is thorough — what banks need. EMI interim option (Wise Business / Revolut Business) — flag as not a full bank account; confirm suitability with accountant. Do not understate the banking timeline.
  #### Step 9: Appoint auditor and set up accounting records

### What it costs (~200 words)
Two tables: (1) formation costs — government fees, expedited option, legal fees, stamp duty on M&AA; (2) annual ongoing costs — registered office, company secretary, auditor, annual return. Use ranges where confirmed. Flag unconfirmed figures with "confirm at time of application."

### Documents required (~150 words)
Table: document / who provides it / format required. Cover directors, shareholders, and the lawyer-prepared documents separately.

### Ongoing obligations after formation (~150 words)
Annual return (HE32) to DRCOR. Audited financial statements (annually, unless exempt). Corporate tax return (TD.4). Brief, practical — not legal detail.

### Frequently asked questions (~200 words)
