## Structure / Outline

### Why renting first is the right move in Paphos (~150 words)
Renting first lets you try areas, understand the market, and meet the rental agreement requirement for the Yellow Slip and tax residency. Buying comes later once you know where you want to be. Short intro — do not over-explain.

### How to find rental properties in Paphos (~200 words)
Bazaraki.com (dominant portal). Local property agents — value of using one in Paphos where good properties move fast. Facebook expat groups for informal market. Why you must view in person before paying anything — rental scams exist.

### What to check before you sign (~300 words)
#### Verify the landlord owns the property
Ask for title deed or Land Registry extract. A lawyer can do a title search for a small fee.
#### What must be in the rental contract
Names, full property address, rent and payment terms, deposit amount and return conditions, tenancy duration, notice periods, maintenance responsibilities, inventory if furnished. Use a checklist format.
#### The 12-month minimum rule for official registrations
If you need the contract for MEU1 (Yellow Slip) or tax residency registration, it must be for at least 12 months. A 6-month transitional lease cannot be used for official registrations.

### Costs: what to budget for (~200 words)
Deposit: 1–2 months' rent. First month on signing. Agent commission: ~1 month + VAT. Stamp duty: abolished from January 2026. Legal review: optional but recommended for complex or high-value lets. Present as a summary table: cost / amount / notes.

### Your rights as a tenant in Cyprus (~150 words)
Eviction process is slow — court proceedings can take up to 5 years. Landlord cannot evict simply to sell mid-tenancy. Deposit return: landlord must return minus documented damages. Rent Control Court / small claims for amounts under €5,000.

### Setting up utilities after you move in (~150 words)
EAC for electricity. Local water board. Gas if applicable. Transfer accounts to your name with rental agreement + passport. Typically 1–5 working days per utility. Link to utilities setup guide when available.
