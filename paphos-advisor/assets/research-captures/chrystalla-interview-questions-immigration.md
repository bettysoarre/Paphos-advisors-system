# Chrystalla Interview Questions — Immigration Processes

These questions were generated by reviewing the six immigration process documents (PROC-IMM-001 through PROC-IMM-006) and identifying every field note that requires real-world validation from a Paphos-based practitioner. Chrystalla is a government liaison with direct experience at the Paphos CRMD office. She is the right person to answer questions about local office practice, real-world document requirements, and what actually happens at the counter.

**What she can answer:** Paphos CRMD office procedures, appointment booking, what officers accept in practice, real-world processing times, common rejection reasons, practical tips.

**What she may not answer:** Tax implications, legal interpretation of EU directives, Investment PR due diligence requirements, company structure advice.

**Note for the interviewer:** Ask for specific examples and stories — "what actually happened" is more useful than what she thinks should happen. Flag any answer that surprises her or contradicts the published guidance.

---

## Section 1 — Paphos CRMD Office: General Practicalities

These questions apply across all immigration routes.

**1.1** How do people currently book appointments at the Paphos CRMD office — is it still by phone and email, or is there an online system now? How far in advance do people need to book for a straightforward application?

**1.2** In April 2026, the CRMD upgraded its case management system and biometric equipment. Has this changed anything in practice at the Paphos office — faster processing, new system for tracking applications, anything notable for clients?

**1.3** How bad are the current processing delays at the Paphos office compared to Limassol and Nicosia? Are there specific times of year when it is busier (summer arrivals, September)?

**1.4** Is a government liaison actually useful for speeding things up, or do they mainly just take away the stress of dealing with the office? What specifically can a liaison do that a client cannot do themselves?

**1.5** Are there informal norms at the Paphos office — morning vs afternoon, certain days of the week, specific officers — that make a real difference to how smoothly an application goes?

## Section 2 — MEU1 Yellow Slip (PROC-IMM-001)

**2.1** We have the official processing time for MEU1 as 2–4 weeks (official) but 1–3 months real-world in Paphos. Is that still accurate as of early 2026, or has it changed?

**2.2** We have documented that rental agreements must be stamped at the Tax Office and that this causes frequent rejections. Is there any flexibility on this in practice — are there officers who accept unstamped agreements? Or is it completely non-negotiable?

**2.3** Is there an officially published minimum financial threshold for the self-sufficient category, or is it genuinely left to officer discretion? What does Chrystalla advise clients to show?

**2.4** On health insurance for MEU1: we have documented that some officers accept GESY registration as proof of health insurance for employed applicants. Is this reliably accepted at Paphos, or does it vary by officer?

**2.5** Are there any document requirements that commonly catch first-time applicants by surprise in Paphos — things not listed on the official checklist?

---

## Section 3 — MEU2 Residence Card (PROC-IMM-002)

**3.1** What is the real-world processing time for MEU2 applications at the Paphos office in 2026? We have seen reports of 10–15 months for some applicants. What is Chrystalla seeing?

**3.2** The MEU2 requires the EU national and the non-EU family member to both attend in person. Are there any exceptions to this in practice — for example, if the EU national is hospitalised, or abroad for work?

**3.3** For the rental agreement: since late 2024, it must be stamped at the Tax Department and certified by the Mukhtar or a notary public. Is the Mukhtar certification always required, or can a notary substitute? Are there any areas of Paphos where the Mukhtar requirement causes practical problems?

**3.4** Is there a published or informal income threshold for the self-sufficient category in the MEU2 context? What figure do practitioners work with for a couple?

**3.5** The April 2026 CRMD system upgrade mentioned online file tracking for applicants. Is this working in practice at Paphos, or are people still having to come in person or call to check status?

**3.6** We have flagged that UK nationals on old paper MEU1/MEU3 certificates must convert to biometric cards by 3 August 2026. Is the Paphos office managing this conversion backlog? Are there delays in booking conversion appointments?

## Section 4 — MEU3 Permanent Residency Certificate (PROC-IMM-003)

**4.1** Is the MEU3 now issued as a biometric card only, or can clients still receive a paper certificate? Is there a preference applicants should state?

**4.2** What is the current appointment booking method for MEU3 applications at the Paphos district office — phone, email, online, or walk-in?

**4.3** What is the most common reason MEU3 applications are delayed or sent back? Is it typically incomplete residency evidence, or something else?

**4.4** For UK nationals covered by the Withdrawal Agreement (resident before 31 December 2020) who want MEU3 — is this being processed as a standard MEU3, or is there a separate procedure? Is the Paphos office handling these consistently?

**4.5** When clients have gaps in their 5-year residency documentation, what is the most practical way to reconstruct the evidence? What does Chrystalla typically help clients source?

## Section 5 — Pink Slip Temporary Residence (PROC-IMM-006)

This section is highest priority for the Paphos business, as UK national retirees (ICP-001) are a key segment.

**5.1** What is the real-world processing time for a Pink Slip application in Paphos in 2026? Is 6–8 months still accurate, or is it longer?

**5.2** What form code is currently used for the Pink Slip application at the Paphos office — MVIS8, MVIS3, MVIS6, or something else? Has this changed recently?

**5.3** Does the principal applicant always have to attend in person at the Paphos office, or can a legal representative / government liaison attend on their behalf?

**5.4** We have the minimum income threshold as €24,000/year (€2,000/month) for a single applicant. Is this applied rigidly, or is there officer discretion? What do applicants need to show for a couple?

**5.5** How long a history of Cyprus bank statements does the Paphos CRMD office require for a first-time Pink Slip application? We have seen "12 months" referenced, but many first-time applicants will not have 12 months of Cyprus bank history. What is the practical minimum?

**5.6** For UK nationals: are they required to provide a bank guarantee (typically €350–€850), or are UK nationals exempt? Has this changed since Brexit?

**5.7** Which UK police clearance certificate is accepted at Paphos for the Pink Slip — the standard DBS certificate, or the ACRO Criminal Records Office certificate, or both?

**5.8** What are the most common reasons Pink Slip applications are rejected or delayed at the Paphos office? What are the top 3 mistakes applicants make?

**5.9** Absence rule: the Pink Slip requires the holder not to be outside Cyprus for more than 3 consecutive months. Is this enforced in practice? Does the Paphos office actually track this, or is it only an issue if something else comes up?

**5.10** At renewal: if a client's income dips below €24,000/year for one year due to a temporary reason (e.g. a pension restructure, or a gap between investment income), is the Paphos office flexible, or is the threshold applied rigidly?

**5.11** What advice does Chrystalla give UK clients about the timeline: when should they first contact her relative to their intended move date? What is the earliest possible move date given the process time?

**5.12** Is there a practical "fastest possible" route through the Pink Slip for a well-prepared UK retiree? What is the absolute minimum time from arriving in Cyprus to holding a valid Pink Slip card?

## Section 8 — Cross-Route Questions

**8.1** For a UK retiree couple arriving in Cyprus today — what is Chrystalla's recommended order of operations? Pink Slip first, then what? What should they do in the first week, first month, first year?

**8.2** What is the interaction between the Pink Slip and the MEU1 in a mixed-nationality household (one EU partner, one non-EU partner)? Can the non-EU partner switch from a Pink Slip to a MEU2 if they marry a subsequent EU national, or is it a fresh application?

**8.3** Are there any new rules, guidance, or changes at the Paphos CRMD office that Chrystalla is aware of that are not yet on the official website? Any changes expected in the next 6 months?

**8.4** For clients who are in a hurry — for example, they have sold their UK home and need to be legally resident in Cyprus within 3 months — what options do they actually have? Is there any way to accelerate the Pink Slip, or should they be managing expectations?

**8.5** What is the biggest misunderstanding that Chrystalla's clients arrive with about the immigration process that she has to correct most often?
